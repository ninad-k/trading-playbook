## Summary

Rob Booker presents a simple, discretionary-mechanical forex system built entirely on three exponential moving averages (5, 13, 62 periods) plotted on a 15- or 30-minute chart. The core thesis is that discipline — entering and exiting exactly when the signal fires, with no hesitation — matters more than the signal itself; the EMA setup is just a vehicle for enforcing that discipline.

## Key points

- Core signal: watch the 5 EMA cross the 13 EMA; a simultaneous cross of both above/below the 62 EMA is a stronger signal.
- A tight, directionless "DNA Spiral" (5 and 13 whipsawing around each other in a narrow range) produces false signals — stay out or trade cautiously.
- The 62 EMA acts as dynamic support/resistance; price returning to touch the 62 after a crossover is a valid secondary entry point.
- Recommended timeframes: 15-minute or 30-minute charts (author has also used 1hr, 4hr, daily, weekly).
- Avoid trading on holidays and avoid entering new positions after 14:00 GMT on Fridays — low/erratic liquidity distorts signals.
- Initial volatility after entry is normal; the trade needs "room" before it's judged a failure.
- Exits are fully mechanical: a stop-loss and/or trailing stop, or a profit target at a recent swing high/low (double top/bottom).

## Actionable rules

1. Buy when the 5 EMA crosses above the 13 EMA (stronger if both also cross above the 62 EMA); sell/short on the mirror-image cross to the downside.
2. Set initial stop-loss 20 pips beyond the 62 EMA; this is not a fixed 20-pip distance from the entry price. (PDF p. 14.)
3. Use a 20-pip trailing stop, or alternatively set a profit target at a recent swing high/low (double top/bottom). The text does not specify a profit threshold for activating the trail. (PDF pp. 14, 17.)
4. Require at least 20 pips of "free room" before judging early adverse movement as a failed trade.
5. Do not enter new trades on U.S. holidays or after 14:00 GMT on Fridays.
6. Treat the 62 EMA touch after a crossover as a valid re-entry (or add-to-position) point for the same trade direction.

## Caveats

Self-published, informally written (heavy use of humor/anecdote), with no backtested statistics, win rate, or sample of trades provided to support the 20-pip parameters. The "5/13/62" combination is not shown to be superior to other EMA periods — the author states he did not invent it and offers no comparative testing.

## Who it is for

Beginner forex traders who want a very simple, rules-based entry/exit framework and want to build the habit of mechanical execution rather than discretionary decision-making.

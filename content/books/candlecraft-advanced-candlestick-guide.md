## Summary

A short, bilingual (Hindi and English, largely parallel text) catalogue of the standard basic candlestick set (Doji variants, Hammer, Hanging Man, Shooting Star, Inverted Hammer, Spinning Top, Marubozu, Engulfing) plus a handful of multi-candle reversal patterns (Morning/Evening Star, Three White Soldiers/Black Crows, Piercing/Dark Cloud Cover, Tweezer Top) and a short chart-pattern section (Bullish/Bearish Flag, Rising/Falling Wedge, Double Top/Bottom, Head and Shoulders, Cup and Handle). Despite its "Advanced" title, every entry is a single short paragraph of definition only — the content and depth match this batch's other beginner-level candlestick glossaries, not an advanced treatment of confluence, multi-timeframe reading, or trade construction.

## Key points

- **Doji family** — Common, Long-legged, Dragonfly, and Gravestone Doji, each defined by where the open/close sit relative to the session's high/low and what indecision or reversal bias that implies.
- **Single-candle reversal/momentum patterns** — Hammer, Hanging Man, Shooting Star, Inverted Hammer, Spinning Top, Bullish/Bearish Marubozu — each given a one-paragraph formation description and general bullish/bearish/indecision label.
- **Multi-candle reversal patterns** — Bullish/Bearish Engulfing, Morning Star, Evening Star, Three White Soldiers, Three Black Crows, Piercing Pattern, Dark Cloud Cover, Tweezer Top.
- **Chart patterns (brief)** — Bullish/Bearish Flag, Rising/Falling Wedge, Double Top, Double Bottom, Head and Shoulders, Cup and Handle — each covered in a short paragraph, several with an explicit continuation- or reversal-pattern label but no target-projection formula.
- **Bilingual, largely duplicated text** — most sections present the same definition twice, once in Hindi and once in English, rather than adding distinct content in each language.

## Actionable rules

None are given. Every pattern in this book is defined only by its visual shape and a general directional bias ("suggests a potential reversal," "indicates strong bullish momentum") — there is no entry trigger, no stop-loss placement, no target, and no required trend context stated for any of the roughly 20 patterns covered. A reader wanting to trade any of these patterns must import entry/stop/target logic from another source; none of it should be assumed from this text.

## Caveats

The book's authorship is genuinely ambiguous in the source material itself: the table-of-contents page is headed "NEW EDITION (2023) — PRESENT BY: VIKAS (FOUNDER OF MERROR TRADER)," the body pages carry a "Merror Trader" footer throughout, but a later contents block and the closing page are instead headed/signed "PRESENT BY: IQ TRADER" and "WRITTEN BY: IQ TRADER," with a separate "WRITTEN BY: VIKAS, FOUNDER OF MERROR TRADER" line appearing just above it. Both names are printed as authorial credits in the same short document, and it is not possible to determine from the text which one is the actual author versus a re-brander or distributor of the other's material — this note lists "IQ Trader" as the byline for consistency with this library's convention of using the most prominently repeated brand name, but the Merror Trader / Vikas attribution is equally present in the source and should not be discounted. Beyond the authorship question, despite its "Advanced" title the book contains no material beyond a basic, standard pattern glossary — no confluence, no multi-timeframe logic, no entry/stop/target rules, and no discussion of what makes any of its patterns more or less reliable in context. It duplicates, pattern-for-pattern, catalogues already covered by this batch's other candlestick titles and by several existing references in this library.

## Who it is for

Only useful as a very short, bilingual refresher on the most basic candlestick shapes; a reader wanting any actionable trading content, or genuinely advanced material despite the title, should look to this batch's `iq-trader-ultimate-trading-playbook` (which at least pairs entries with entry/stop/exit boxes) or to this library's more established candlestick references instead.

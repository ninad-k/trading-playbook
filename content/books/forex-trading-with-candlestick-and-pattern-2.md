## Summary

Part 2 of this Forex Systems Research course adds pivots, Fibonacci levels, indicators, money management, and a trading plan to the candlestick and chart-pattern material developed in Part 1. Its CI System is deliberately discretionary: higher-timeframe structure and manually drawn levels define the setting, a candlestick pattern supplies the immediate setup, and stochastic direction or a moving-average crossover confirms momentum. The value lies in combining independent evidence around a price zone rather than following any one indicator (PDF pp. 1-24).

## Key points

- Daily pivots derive from the prior high, low, and close; P, R1-R3, and S1-S3 become candidate targets and reversal zones (PDF p. 3).
- Pivot levels lag badly in strong trends and should be checked against trendlines and Fibonacci levels (PDF pp. 4-6).
- The preferred retracements are 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, and 76.4%; projections include 138.2%, 161.8%, and 261.8% (PDF p. 7).
- Indicators filter price setups. The source uses 15,3,3 stochastic on 5- and 15-minute charts, 8,3,3 on hourly charts, and 5/13 EMAs on the two faster charts (PDF pp. 13, 15, 22-23).
- The hourly Bollinger Bands use 20 periods and two standard deviations (PDF pp. 14, 22-23).
- Position risk is tied to the initial stop and capped by the course at 7% of account equity, an aggressive historical threshold (PDF pp. 16, 24).

## Actionable rules

1. Before trading, inspect weekly, daily, four-hour, and hourly candles; draw nearby pivots, support/resistance, trendlines, and Fibonacci levels (PDF p. 23).
2. Enter on a 15-minute engulfing candle only when stochastic has crossed and points in the trade direction. A later 5/13 EMA cross on the five-minute chart is secondary confirmation (PDF p. 23).
3. Place a long stop below nearby support and a short stop above nearby resistance, using a trendline, pivot, Fibonacci level, or three-bar pivot. The source favors a potential reward at least twice the planned risk (risk:reward of 1:2 or better; PDF pp. 23-24).
4. Exit on an opposite engulfing candle, a rejection from a marked level, a Fibonacci target, or an adverse 5/13 EMA cross (PDF p. 24).

## Caveats

The full OCR is readable, but chart labels have minor errors. The examples are selected, the entries remain discretionary, and no audited expectancy is supplied. The 7% single-trade cap is unusually high, while platform, session, and execution details reflect 2003.

## Who it is for

Forex traders who can interpret candlesticks and want a structured confluence checklist rather than a mechanical signal.

## Summary

Written by a day-trading coach, this manual introduces direct-access trading mechanics (level 2, NASDAQ/NYSE order routing, ECNs, SOES) and a set of price-action setups for short-term equity trading. It emphasizes reading order flow and volume over indicators, and closes with money-management and psychology rules distilled from coaching many traders.

## Key points

- **Level 2 / order routing** — bid/ask columns show competing market makers and ECNs; active vs. passive orders determine fill speed.
- **Multiple timeframes** — always check an intraday chart against the daily chart before entering; trade only when both agree.
- **Core setups** — breakout from consolidation, the "pivot" reversal, three-bar continuation pattern, stochastic/MA crossover reversals (1-min chart), basic swing pullback buy/sell.
- **Candlestick reversal signals** — doji, hammer, shooting star used mainly after a significant move into support/resistance, not in sideways markets.
- **Volume confirmation** — rising volume on breakouts confirms; stalling volume signals a weak, trend-less move.
- **Whole-number support/resistance** — round numbers ($50, $100) act as psychological stop/target levels, sometimes overriding technical stops.
- **Trade management** — four exit tools: initial stop, partial profit-taking, breakeven stop, trailing stop.
- **Learning progression** — paper trade until 75%+ win rate, then trade small size (under 100 shares) before scaling up.

## Actionable rules

1. Moving averages used: 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200-period; 20 and 200 are most important on any timeframe.
2. Breakout entry: buy when price breaks above the high of a consolidation after a prior uptrend; stop below the consolidation low or a relevant MA.
3. Pivot reversal (15-min chart): buy when current candle's high exceeds the prior candle's high after a down move of at least $2; stop below the prior candle's low.
4. Stochastic reversal (1-min chart): standard 14,1,3 settings; buy on cross above the 20 band from an oversold extreme, sell on cross below the 80 band.
5. MA crossover reversal (1-min chart): buy when the 5MA crosses above the 10MA in an uptrend pullback; vice versa for shorts.
6. Risk per trade: never risk more than 2% of trading capital on a single trade.
7. Day-trading account minimum cited: $25,000 (U.S. pattern-day-trader rule); standard commission cited as $14.95/trade.

## Caveats

Content reflects early-2000s U.S. equity market structure (SOES, SuperDOT, pre-decimalization commissions) that no longer applies directly to modern electronic markets. Advice is anecdotal and self-published, with no backtested statistics behind the setups; risk disclaimers note results are not guaranteed.

## Who it is for

Beginner-to-intermediate discretionary day traders who want an introduction to level 2 tape reading and simple, discretionary price-action setups rather than a systematic or quantitative approach.

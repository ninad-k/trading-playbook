## Summary

A compilation of Stockscores.com educational strategy articles by Tyler Bollhorn, each structured around a concept, screening criteria (built for the Stockscores Market Scan tool), and a visual/chart-based confirmation step. The pieces covered include "Bottom Fishing" (buying reversing downtrends), a support-and-resistance primer, "Profiting from Euphoria" (shorting/exiting overextended spike moves), and an "Adoption Cycle of Stocks" essay mapping a stock's psychological life cycle from consolidation through euphoric top to renewed base.

## Key points

- Bottom Fishing strategy defines three stages of a reversal: a break from a declining trendline (break from pessimism), a rising-bottom consolidation above the prior low (show of confidence), and a breakout from that rising bottom (show of optimism); traders can watch-list stocks in stage 1-2 or wait for stage 3 confirmation.
- Bottom Fishing scan criteria: bullish candle, bullish short-term moving average but bearish medium-term moving average, price ≥25% below its 150-day high, bullish Williams Volume Accumulation and Volume Price Trend, and minimum $250,000 dollar-volume for liquidity.
- Support is a price floor strengthened by repeated tests; resistance is a price ceiling strengthened the same way; both tend to cluster at round dollar values because they act as psychological anchors for participants.
- A breakout through resistance on above-average volume tends to be followed by continuation; failure to hold above a broken resistance level (or a support level) warns the move may fail.
- "Profiting from Euphoria" targets stocks up ≥25% in 30 days, with volume above the 150-day average, an overbought RSI reading, high/increasing volatility, and a bearish (red) candle — signaling exhaustion of an emotion-driven spike ripe for a pullback.
- Both strategies explicitly warn they are not 100% effective and instruct traders to take losses early if the market invalidates the setup (e.g., a bearish candle followed by renewed strength should be respected as a stop-out signal).
- The Adoption Cycle essay maps a repeatable psychological cycle: consolidation, breakout on fundamental change, a shallow post-breakout pullback, a profit-taking pullback mid-trend, a euphoric blow-off top, a "sucker rally" failure to make new highs, then breakdown into a downtrend as late holders still cite "the fundamentals."

## Actionable rules

1. Bottom Fishing entry filter: stock ≥25% below its 150-day high, bullish short-term MA crossing against a still-bearish medium-term MA, bullish volume-accumulation indicators, $250,000+ daily dollar volume.
2. Bottom Fishing exit/invalidation: caution or exit if price falls back below the resistance level it just broke (former resistance failing to act as new support).
3. Profiting from Euphoria entry filter: ≥25% price gain in the trailing 30 days, volume above the 150-day average, RSI overbought, high and rising 20-day volatility, bearish (red) candle making a new high intraday before closing down.
4. Profiting from Euphoria invalidation: skip or exit the short if the bearish candle is followed by a bullish day, or if the bearish candle failed to print a new high (suggesting a mere pause, not a top).
5. No universal position-sizing or stop-percentage figures are given; risk control is expressed as "take your loss and move on" when price invalidates the pattern.

## Caveats

The scan criteria are built specifically for the proprietary Stockscores.com Market Scan tool and its named indicators (Williams Volume Accumulation, Volume Price Trend), which readers without that platform will need to replicate manually. Published in 2000, so examples and volume/dollar thresholds reflect that era's typical stock prices and liquidity; no formal backtest statistics are given for either strategy.

## Who it is for

Swing traders comfortable with chart-based pattern reading and volume/momentum screening who want concrete, filter-based setups for both reversal entries (bottom fishing) and fading momentum exhaustion (euphoria fade).

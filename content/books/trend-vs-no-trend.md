## Summary

Dolan frames the trend/no-trend paradox as a tool-selection problem: trend indicators can fail in ranges, while oscillators can generate premature countertrend signals in persistent trends (p. 1). He recommends starting with trendlines on daily and weekly charts, then carrying important support and resistance into shorter timeframes (p. 2). The DMI system adds an objective filter: ADX above 25 indicates a trend, below 20 indicates no trend, and DI+ or DI− crossovers supply directional signals. Wilder’s extreme-point rule confirms a crossover only when the relevant high or low is breached in the next period (p. 2). When ADX is above 25, MACD exits and divergences should be discounted; when ADX falls below 25, momentum tools become more useful (pp. 2–3). In the EUR/USD example, a short is taken near 1.3060 after ADX falls below 25 and MACD weakens, then exited near 1.2900 as MACD turns up. In USD/CHF, a long near 1.1650 is held through premature MACD exits until trendline support breaks and ADX falls below 25 near 1.2000 (p. 3). The article says only about one-third of historical currency price action is trending, but offers no dataset details (p. 1).

## Key points

- Use trend tools to classify regime before interpreting momentum signals.
- ADX measures trend strength, not direction; DI lines provide direction (p. 2).

## Actionable rules

1. Mark daily and weekly trendlines before shorter-period levels; use ADX above 25 as the article’s trend regime and below 20 as its no-trend regime (pp. 1–2).
2. Confirm a bullish DI crossover only after price exceeds that crossover period’s high, and a bearish crossover only after price breaks that period’s low (p. 2).
3. Favor trend-following signals while ADX is above 25; reconsider momentum signals when ADX falls below 25. These are the article’s example thresholds, not tested universal constants (pp. 2–3).

## Caveats

The examples are selected charts and thresholds are conventional. No formal performance test, costs, or robustness analysis is provided.

## Who it is for

Traders building regime-aware indicator rules.

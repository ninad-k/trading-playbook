## Summary

A short, promotional but concrete guide from the founder of MrSwing.com describing his "Master Plan": a fully mechanical entry/exit system for swing trading stocks over a few days to a few weeks. The pitch is that swing trading fills the gap between buy-and-hold (too slow, too exposed to drawdowns) and day trading (too demanding to watch all day) by capturing pullbacks within an established trend using preset orders that require no intraday judgment.

## Key points

- **Stock filter** — minimum price ~$7-12 and average daily volume ≥500,000 shares, to avoid thin, easily manipulated names.
- **Uptrend/downtrend identification** — a series of higher highs and higher lows (uptrend) or lower highs and lower lows (downtrend); trade the pullback in an uptrend (buy) or the pull-up in a downtrend (sell short).
- **Indicator stack** — 10/20/50-day simple moving averages (price above the 10- and 20-day, with 10-day above 20-day, confirms uptrend); a 3-day/13-day moving average of Alexander Elder's Force Index; the Directional Movement Index (ADX>30 with +DI>−DI confirms trend).
- **Position sizing** — capital is divided into ~15 (or up to 20) equal trade-sized buckets; unfilled orders simply don't execute rather than triggering margin calls.
- **Entry timing** — orders placed shortly after the open; if the stock opens within $0.50 of the prior close, enter minutes after the open; if it gaps ≥$0.50 in the trade's direction, wait 30 minutes; if it gaps against the trade, wait only 5 minutes.
- **Entry trigger** — buy stop 6 cents above the prior day's high (or the current day's high after a gap).
- **Profit/stop levels** — sell limit for half the position at +7% from entry; stop-loss at the greater of −4% or 6 cents below the relevant day's low; the stop is trailed daily (never loosened) once triggered conditions are met.
- **Short swing** — mirror-image rules for downtrends; requires a margin-approved account even if margin itself isn't used.

## Actionable rules

1. Enter long via buy-stop 6 cents above the prior day's (or gap day's) high.
2. Take profit on half the position at +7%; let the remainder run under a trailing stop.
3. Initial stop-loss: max(4% below entry, 6 cents below the entry day's low).
4. Trail the stop daily to 6 cents below each new day's low, moving it only upward (long) or downward (short).
5. Abandon an unfilled order after 5 trading days.

## Caveats

The book doubles as marketing for the author's SwingTracker software and MasterSwings subscription service, and includes unverified testimonials. The 7%/4% targets and $0.50 gap threshold are fixed, non-adaptive numbers tuned to a particular (unspecified, likely mid-2000s) market regime and price level — they may need rescaling for different volatility conditions or instruments.

## Who it is for

Beginner-to-intermediate swing traders who want a fully mechanical, discretion-free rule set (specific stop/target percentages and order types) rather than a discussion of swing-trading theory.

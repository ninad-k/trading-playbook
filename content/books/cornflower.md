## Summary

Cornflower is a forex EMA template. The 72 EMA defines the dominant trend, the 24 EMA approximates the daily trend on an hourly chart, the 12 EMA represents a half-day trend, and the 8 EMA tracks faster intraday movement (p. 1). Long setups require the shorter EMAs above the 72 EMA; shorts require them below. Conservative entries wait for price to pull back toward the 12 or 24 EMA on H1. Aggressive entries use M30 after a decisive move beyond the 72 EMA with the shorter averages pointing in the same direction (pp. 1-2). The stated initial objective is 20 pips.

## Key points

- H1 is the conservative chart and M30 the aggressive chart (pp. 1-2).
- The preferred long stack is 8 > 12 > 24 above 72; shorts reverse the order (p. 2).
- Pullback entries may be mechanical or discretionary around the 12/24 EMA area (p. 2).
- The source says shorter periods whipsaw and longer periods lag (p. 2).
- A 5-minute chart may refine timing but is not the core template (p. 2).

## Actionable rules

1. Plot 8, 12, 24, and 72 EMAs on H1 or M30 and remove the grid for clarity (p. 1).
2. For a conservative pullback, require 8/12/24 all above or below 72 and price at 12 or 24 EMA; target 20 pips or manage by trend strength (p. 2).
3. For an aggressive entry, require a strong candle beyond 72 EMA and aligned shorter averages; target 20 pips and hold only if a trend develops (p. 2).

## Caveats

The system is a two-page, self-reported forex method with no expectancy study. It gives no complete stop or position-sizing formula, and claims about catching every major move are not demonstrated.

## Who it is for

Experienced forex traders testing EMA trend and pullback rules with realistic costs.

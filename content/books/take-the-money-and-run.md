## Summary

A short free e-book describing a manual EUR/USD scalping method: watch a 1-minute chart with 21-period/2-standard-deviation Bollinger Bands and 14-period RSI, and take small (3-10 pip) counter-extension trades when price pierces a Bollinger Band while RSI is simultaneously overbought or oversold. The author (using an Oanda demo account) restricts trading to specific low-spread session windows and stops after netting roughly 10 pips for the day.

## Key points

- Setup: EUR/USD, 1-minute OHLC chart, Bollinger Bands (21, 2), RSI (14), traded on a broker with a small (≈1.5 pip) spread.
- Trading windows only: 2am-5am EST, 8am-12pm EST, and 7:30pm-10pm EST.
- Avoid trading 30 minutes before and 1 hour after high-impact ("orange" or "red") economic news releases for EUR/USD.
- Buy signal: price closes/extends below the lower Bollinger Band while RSI drops below 30.
- Sell signal: price extends above the upper Bollinger Band while RSI rises above 70.
- Target: the Bollinger Band centerline, typically 3-5 pips; stop the trading day after netting about 10 pips.
- Advanced filter: after a strong prevailing trend, wait for price to cross the Bollinger centerline before taking a counter-trend entry, to confirm the trend has actually paused.
- Author reports roughly 80% win rate on the base system, improving to about 90% with the advanced trend filter (unverified, anecdotal).

## Actionable rules

1. Trade only EUR/USD, only within the three specified session windows, avoiding the news blackout window.
2. Enter long when price pierces the lower Bollinger Band (21,2) and RSI(14) < 30; enter short when price pierces the upper band and RSI > 70.
3. Stop-loss: fixed 5 pips on every trade.
4. Take-profit: aim for the Bollinger centerline, typically 3-5 pips; exit manually rather than waiting for a further move.
5. Position size: risk no more than 3-5% of account equity per trade (author's stated practice, e.g., $500 risk on a $10,000 account with a 5-pip stop ≈ 1 standard lot).
6. Stop trading for the day after reaching roughly 10 pips of net profit; do not try to "win back" a losing day.

## Caveats

This is a free, informally-written promotional e-book with no verified track record, backtest, or sample size beyond a handful of illustrated screenshots; the claimed 80-90% win rate is anecdotal. A fixed 5-pip stop with a similar-sized target implies a roughly 1:1 reward-to-risk system that depends entirely on a high win rate to be profitable, and transaction costs (spread) consume a meaningful share of a 3-5 pip target. The "$4,000/month for 2 hours' work" framing in the introduction is an unsubstantiated income claim.

## Who it is for

Beginner forex traders wanting a simple, fully mechanical mean-reversion scalp with explicit entry, exit, and stop rules to practice on a demo account; not a substitute for independent backtesting before risking real capital.

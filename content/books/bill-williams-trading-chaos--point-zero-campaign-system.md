## What it is

A discretionary, Elliott-wave-driven campaign system for entering a new trend near its start and adding to the position as it develops, then exiting on a confluence-based exhaustion signal. Unlike the Alligator-gated system of the sequel book (*New Trading Dimensions*), this earlier version has no moving-average envelope filter — timing comes from Elliott wave counting, the Market Facilitation Index (MFI), and the fractal/thumb price patterns, layered into one "campaign" rather than a single all-at-once entry.

## Rules

1. **Find Point Zero.** Identify the end of a completed 5-wave Elliott sequence (a-through-e correction finished) with reasonable confidence. This anchors the whole campaign; do not initiate without it.
2. **First entry (wave 1).** Standard fractal breakout: buy stop one tick above (sell stop one tick below) the middle bar of a 5-bar fractal forming off Point Zero. In a ranging market, use the 3-bar "thumb" variant instead — valid only when 2 of its 3 bars are squat (unusually low range for their volume) or green (rising volume vs. the prior bar) — entering one tick beyond the thumb bar's extreme.
3. **Wave-3 add.** Target wave 3's end on a smaller timeframe via a confluence of the five magic bullets (below). Initial length target: 1x–1.62x wave 1; rarely shorter.
4. **Wave-4 add.** Do not add for wave 5 until the 5/34 (or 5/34/5) momentum oscillator crosses back through zero. Corroborate with a completed a-b-c/triangle correction and a Fibonacci time projection (wave 4 tends to end 1.38x–1.62x the wave-1-to-wave-3 time span, from the wave-2 low). Add once wave 4 is judged complete.
5. **Exit / campaign close.** Treat a reversal as high-confidence only once 2+ of the five magic bullets align: MFI divergence against price, price inside the Elliott retracement/target zone, a fractal at the extreme, a squat bar at the extreme, a momentum-direction change. Close the full campaign, not just the latest add.
6. **Position sizing.** Not committed all at once — a worked soybean example scales in at the wave-1 length and again at ~110% of it, trailing stops to the prior wave's extreme as each leg confirms.

## Risk

No fixed percentage-of-equity or dollar-per-trade formula is given; sizing follows Elliott levels instead of a formula. Two independent stops run together:
- **Structure stop**: at the extreme of the triggering pattern (fractal/thumb bar, or the prior Elliott wave's high/low), tightened as each leg confirms.
- **Air bag stop**: a second, higher-timeframe stop one tick beyond the high/low of the second-back bar on a significantly higher timeframe (an approximate 1:5 ratio between adjacent timeframes), meant to survive gaps and violent single-bar spikes a normal stop would not.

The worked example gives real figures: a per-contract risk of $458 on an early leg, and a closed-out profit of $1,275 per contract on part of the position — risk is evaluated leg-by-leg as the campaign builds, not fixed at the outset.

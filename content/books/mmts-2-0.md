## Summary

A self-published forex day-trading course (Monika Korzec, korzec.ca) built entirely around GBP/USD price action in two recurring daily windows: the US 8:30am ET news release and the London open. It combines a compounding money-management plan, a set of six numbered "setups" derived from how the 8:15/8:30/8:45 candles form relative to each other, a supplementary MKS trendline overlay, and a separate simpler system for the London open, plus contributed "technical exit" chapters from two other traders.

## Key points

- **Money management target** — aim for 10-20 pips/day; the book models compounding from $500 starting capital adding a mini-lot every ~10 winning days, reaching over $100,000 in about a simulated year under the stated assumptions.
- **8:15/8:30/8:45 candle framework** — the core setups are built from the spread (high-low, not open-close) of the 15-minute candles from 8:15-8:30am and 8:30-8:45am ET, and the combined 8:15-8:45 range.
- **No-trade filters** — skip the trade if the 8:15, 8:30, and combined-range spreads are all equal; if the 8:15-8:45 range is ≤14 pips; if the 8:30 candle spread is ≤10 pips; or if both the 8:15 and 8:30 candle spreads are ≤13 pips.
- **MMTS 1 rule (fallback/baseline)** — buy 4 pips above (or sell 4 pips below) the high/low of the 8:30-9:00am range, whichever triggers first; skip the system entirely if any 15-minute bar in that window exceeds 50 pips.
- **Setup 1 (primary)** — enter 1 pip above the 8:15-8:45 high (long) or 1 pip below the 8:15-8:45 low (short), whichever triggers first, only if it happens before 9:00am.
- **Setups 2-6** — Setup 2 is a dedicated NFP-day rule (never in May); Setups 3-6 are pattern-based (three-candle shapes resembling "123 Top"/"123 Bottom" or specific close-price configurations) that trigger an entry at the 8:45 candle's close.
- **Target/stop** — standard target is 19 pips; stop is 20 pips (both candles' spreads under 40 pips), 25 pips (8:15 spread less than half the 8:30 spread), 45 pips (reverse case), or 40 pips (either spread over 40 pips); exit near breakeven if 4-5 consecutive bars trade in a tight, non-trending range.
- **London Open system** — separate and simpler: enter at the close of the 3:15am ET bar based on the pattern of the 2:45/3:00/3:15 candles, fixed 30-pip target and 50-pip stop; gets "extra strength" when it agrees with the direction of a companion MKS-2 trendline.
- **Trendline confluence rule** — if the New York setup and the MKS trendline disagree on direction, the author recommends skipping the trade.
- **Tools recommended** — a well-regulated broker with tight spreads and good tick data; Fibonacci Trader charting software is the author's stated preference.

## Actionable rules

1. Compute the 8:15-8:30 and 8:30-8:45 candle spreads (high-low) on GBP/USD 15-minute charts every NY news morning.
2. Skip the trade under any of the no-trade filter conditions listed above.
3. Default entry (Setup 1): buy 1 pip above / sell 1 pip below the 8:15-8:45 high/low, valid only until 9:00am ET; if it doesn't trigger, fall back to Setup 4, Setup 6, or the MMTS 1 rule.
4. Target 19 pips; set the stop per the spread-comparison rule above (20/25/40/45 pips depending on relative candle sizes).
5. For NFP-day Setup 2 specifically: skip in May; skip if the 8:15 candle is a doji, <17 pips, or >40 pips; otherwise enter at 8:30 in the direction of the 8:15 candle's color, with an 85-pip stop and 75-pip target.
6. London Open: enter at the 3:15am bar close per the 2:45/3:00/3:15 candle pattern; use a 30-pip target and 50-pip stop; skip if the setup conflicts with the MKS-2 trendline direction.

## Caveats

This is a self-published retail course with informal production (garbled OCR text in places, hand-drawn figures referenced but not fully described in the extracted text) and no independent verification of its stated 2005 backtest results (e.g., "1,713 pips for the year" mechanical entries). The money-management compounding table assumes an unbroken string of 10-pip winning days, which is an idealized illustration rather than a realistic equity curve. "123 Top"/"123 Bottom" pattern setups (3-6) are described visually via figures not reproducible here in detail — a trader would need the original chart images to apply them precisely.

## Who it is for

Discretionary/semi-mechanical GBP/USD day traders who want a fully time-boxed routine (specific 15-minute windows around NY news and the London open) with defined stops and targets, and are willing to backtest the setup rules themselves before trading live.

## Summary

A reference sheet, not a full trading system: it defines candlestick construction (body = open/close range, wicks/shadows = high/low extremes, color indicates direction) and then catalogs roughly 35 individual and multi-candle patterns, pairing each pattern's visual definition with a one-line interpretation (bullish/bearish, reversal/continuation).

## Key points

- **Single-candle reversal signals** — Doji (open ≈ close, more significant after a strong prior move), Hammer (bullish reversal in a downtrend), Hanging Man (bearish reversal in an uptrend), Shooting Star (bearish reversal), Gravestone Doji (bearish top signal).
- **Engulfing patterns** — Bullish/Bearish Engulfing Lines: a small body fully contained within, and then engulfed by, an opposite-color larger body signals a reversal.
- **Harami patterns** — a large body followed by a small opposite-color body contained within it; the "Harami Cross" variant (small body is a doji) is a stronger reversal signal.
- **Star patterns** — Morning Star / Evening Star (and their Doji-Star variants) are three-candle bottom/top reversal signals with a gapping middle candle.
- **Continuation patterns** — Bullish 3 / Bearish 3 (long body, several small bodies, another long body in the same direction), Separating Lines, Rising/Falling Windows (gaps expected to act as support/resistance on a retest).
- **Multi-candle trend-exhaustion signals** — Three Black Crows (bearish top reversal), Three White Soldiers (bullish bottom reversal), Tweezer Tops/Bottoms (matching highs/lows = minor reversal).
- Body-only definitions: Black Body = bearish (close < open), White Body = bullish (close > open); "Big" variants of each indicate a wide range with opens/closes near the extremes.

## Actionable rules

None given as position-sizing or entry/exit rules — this is a pattern-identification glossary rather than a complete trading system. The document's implicit rule set is pattern recognition itself: match the candle shape to the closest catalog entry and treat that entry's "interpretation" line as the signal (e.g., Piercing Line = bottom reversal signal when a black candle is followed by a white candle opening below the black candle's low but closing more than 50% into its body).

## Caveats

No author or publication date attributed to the source document; no discussion of context requirements (trend location, volume confirmation, or timeframe) beyond brief notes on individual patterns (e.g., doji significance depends on occurring after a strong move). Should be paired with a full trading system for entries, stops, and targets — this document only classifies shapes.

## Who it is for

Beginners who need a quick illustrated reference for identifying and naming candlestick patterns while reading price charts.

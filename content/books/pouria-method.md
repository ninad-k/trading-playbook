## Summary

This is a single-page indicator cheat sheet, not a book — it is reproduced here in full because it is genuinely this short. It defines three moving averages (two red linear-weighted averages of the low, periods 85 and 75; one yellow exponential average of the close, period 5) plotted alongside a custom MACD (fast EMA 15, slow EMA 26, signal SMA 1), applied to 30-minute and 1-hour charts. The entire trading logic is a single sentence for each direction: short when the yellow line crosses down through both red lines with MACD confirming down; long on the mirror-image up-cross. Risk is fixed at a maximum 14-pip stop, with a 10-15 pip profit target.

## Key points

- **Indicator set**: WMA(85) on low, WMA(75) on low (both linear-weighted, plotted in red), and EMA(5) on close (yellow).
- **MACD settings**: fast EMA 15, slow EMA 26, signal SMA 1 — a non-standard, very fast signal-line smoothing.
- **Timeframe**: designed for 30-minute and 1-hour charts only.
- **Short signal**: yellow EMA(5) crosses downward through both red WMA lines, confirmed by MACD also turning downward.
- **Long signal**: yellow EMA(5) crosses upward through both red WMA lines, confirmed by MACD also turning upward.
- **Fixed risk**: stop-loss capped at 14 pips maximum (the author notes skepticism that a loss of that size would even occur).
- **Fixed target**: take profit at 10 to 15 pips, with an explicit "don't be greedy" instruction not to hold for more.

## Actionable rules

1. Plot WMA(85, low), WMA(75, low), and EMA(5, close) plus MACD(15,26,1) on a 30-minute or 1-hour chart.
2. Short when the EMA(5) line crosses down through both WMA lines and MACD confirms with a downward move.
3. Long when the EMA(5) line crosses up through both WMA lines and MACD confirms with an upward move.
4. Set stop-loss at a maximum of 14 pips.
5. Take profit at 10-15 pips; do not hold out for more.

## What it is

The MTPredictor "TS" set-up (TS1, TS2, etc. in the software and daily reports) is a reversal system built from three conditions that must all align: (1) an obvious Elliott wave count is in place, (2) price has reached a Fibonacci-ratio Wave Price Target (WPT) zone consistent with that count, and (3) price action confirms the reversal via a coloured Reversal Bar and a break of its extreme. The method explicitly refuses to act on ambiguous wave counts even when price sits at a technically-derived level.

## Rules

1. **Establish a valid Elliott wave count.** Use the software's "Show Elliott Waves" module (Minor/Intermediate/Major degree), or manually label using Intermediate-degree connected pivots. Verify: wave 3 not shortest of 1/3/5, wave 4 doesn't overlap wave 1, waves 2 and 4 alternate in structure. If no clean count exists, don't trade this chart — a valid pattern exists only about half the time.
2. **Project the WPT for the wave expected to end**, using the core ratios (0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.786, 1.00, 1.272, 1.618, 2.618, 4.236) applied to a defined swing set per wave label: Wave 1orA and 2orB WPTs need 2 clicks each (the single preceding swing, or the swing off a completed wave C); Wave 3, 4, and C WPTs need 3 clicks; Wave 5 needs 5; Wave E needs 5 (full ABCDE). Best zones are clusters where multiple ratios, or multiple wave degrees at once, converge in a small area.
3. **Wait for a coloured Reversal Bar inside the WPT zone** — blue (buy) or red (sell). A near-miss (price exceeding the zone by up to about half its width, or closing back inside it) still counts.
4. **Enter on confirmation**: buy above the Reversal Bar's high, or sell below its low. An unconfirmed Reversal Bar is not traded.
5. **Filter every candidate through the risk/reward check** before committing: project profit to the first target versus the initial stop distance (Initial Risk to Reward module, or the 2-3 zone as a quick visual proxy). Minimum acceptable ratio: 2:1 — skip the trade otherwise, regardless of pattern quality.
6. **Optional mid-trend adds** (same 2:1 filter applies): a minor pullback — a single-swing correction not exceeding the prior swing extreme, entered on a break of its high/low — or inside/outside-day and WPT-filtered 80/20-day triggers layered onto the same wave count.

## Risk

Initial stop sits just beyond the triggering Reversal Bar's (or pullback bar's) extreme — a pattern-defined level, not a fixed percentage or dollar distance. The book's worked example risks a fixed 3% of account equity per position, applied identically to the initial entry and each pyramided add-on. Minimum 2:1 profit-to-risk is required before entry; the 2-3 zone (profit ≈ 2–3× initial risk) is then used to bank partial profit or tighten the stop as the trade matures — avoid parking stops at obvious minor swing points where noise triggers them prematurely. Additional units are only pyramided in the direction of the already-established larger-degree trend, never against the Step 1 wave count.

## Caveats

The system depends on subjective judgment at the entry gate — deciding whether a wave count is "clear and obvious enough" — despite otherwise mechanical WPT/stop rules. Worked examples are hand-picked historical charts with no aggregate backtested win rate, R-multiple, or drawdown reported. Manual WPT clustering without the software is described as impractically tedious for the multi-swing wave 5 and E targets. The 80/20 day is adapted from Connors and Raschke's "Street Smarts" and needs real-time, day-session-only monitoring. No maximum-drawdown or losing-streak guidance is given beyond the 3%-of-equity risk cap.

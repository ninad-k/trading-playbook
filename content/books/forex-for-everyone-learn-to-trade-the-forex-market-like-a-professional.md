## Summary

This 2005 course organizes intraday forex trading around price's relationship to 200-, 75-, and 21-period moving averages. The 200-period simple moving average defines broad bullish or bearish mode; retracements to the 75-period SMA or 21-period EMA distinguish normal and fast trends. The trader classifies each session into one of six day types, then looks for double tops or bottoms, reversal bars, support and resistance, trendlines, and retracements that fit the current regime (PDF pp. 4-9, 60-65).

## Key points

- The daily process starts with scheduled news, current conditions, chart location, and a day-type classification (PDF p. 4).
- The six regimes are slow, normal, and fast trends; big and small ranges; and news trading (PDF pp. 8-9).
- Moving averages are explicitly treated as lagging descriptions, not predictors (PDF p. 7).
- A sampled setup enters after a double top or bottom forms near a meaningful level and a reversal bar confirms it (PDF pp. 18, 27, 56).
- A three-bar stop trails behind consecutive lower highs in a short; the illustrated buffer is five pips above the third bar (PDF p. 41).
- Range and news conditions require more confirmation because large bars and false breaks can overwhelm a normal setup (PDF p. 84).

## Actionable rules

1. Determine whether price is oriented to the 200 SMA, 75 SMA, or 21 EMA before selecting rules; change the plan when that relationship changes.
2. In the sampled fast downtrend rules, short only after a double top or resistance test plus a reversal bar, with an initial stop ten pips above that level (PDF p. 79).
3. Trail with a three-bar stop; if the current bar is at least 20 pips, the course permits a one-bar stop five pips above it for a short (PDF p. 79).
4. Exit at the stop, a confirmed support/double-bottom reversal during a major news window, or the stated 50-pip objective. Do not countertrend a fast-trend day (PDF p. 79).

## Caveats

Only 28 OCR-sampled pages were available, so several day-type procedures and the full long/short symmetry were not inspected. OCR is compressed around charts. The course's performance sample is self-reported and not independently tested.

## Who it is for

Intraday forex traders studying how to adapt pattern entries and stops to market regimes.

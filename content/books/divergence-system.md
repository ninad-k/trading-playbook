## Summary

A free promotional e-book (distributed to market a paid course) that first walks through the underlying indicator theory — support/resistance, supply/demand, MACD crossovers and divergence, Stochastic, RSI, and Williams %R — then presents a single rule: enter on a divergence between price and an oscillator, and exit (and reverse) on the opposite divergence. The system trades the 4-hour chart for signals, confirmed on the 1-hour chart, and is illustrated with a walk-through of six sequential trades on USD/CHF from August–September 2008.

## Key points

- **Classic divergence** — price makes a new high/low that the oscillator fails to confirm (2–3 occurrences = "triple divergence"); most significant at overbought/oversold extremes.
- **Serial divergence** — the same pattern extended across more than three consecutive highs/lows.
- Primary signal indicator: MACD divergence on the 240-minute (4H) chart.
- Confirmation indicators: Stochastic Oscillator on the 4H chart, plus RSI and Williams %R on the 60-minute (1H) chart.
- Williams %R: readings of 80–100% signal oversold, 0–20% signal overbought (inverted scale).
- Core rule: enter on one type of divergence (e.g., bullish), exit and simultaneously reverse into the opposite position when the opposite (bearish) divergence appears.
- Recommended stop-loss: 60–100 pips, sized for carrying positions overnight.
- Worked example scales position size up as account equity grows (using 10% of current equity per trade), turning a 1,851-pip/39-day result into a larger dollar return than fixed lot sizing.

## Actionable rules

1. Identify divergence between price and MACD on the 4H chart (price makes new high/low, MACD does not confirm).
2. Confirm with Stochastic on 4H and with RSI + Williams %R on the 1H chart before entering.
3. Enter in the direction the divergence implies (bearish MACD divergence → sell; bullish → buy).
4. Set a stop-loss of 60–100 pips.
5. Exit (and reverse) the position when the opposite divergence appears on the same indicator stack.
6. Position size: risk roughly 10% of current account equity per trade (author's worked example, not independently verified).

## Caveats

This is a free lead-magnet for the author's paid "5emas Forex Trading System" and "Building Millions on FOREX" e-book, and its profitability case study is a single, cherry-picked 39-day, 6-trade sequence rather than a statistically meaningful backtest — no win rate, drawdown, or out-of-sample data is given. The position-sizing example (compounding into larger lots after each win) illustrates favorable-case math rather than a tested money-management rule, and its final "$10,000 into $1,000,000+ in 24 months" pitch for the upsell product is an unsubstantiated marketing claim.

## Who it is for

Forex traders wanting a simple, mechanical, multi-timeframe divergence framework combining MACD, Stochastic, RSI, and Williams %R; best read skeptically given the promotional framing and thin evidence base.

Everything else on this site is a reference. You look something up, you read the note, you leave. That
is useful once you already know what you are looking for — and useless when you don't.

This is the other thing. It is a course, and it runs in one direction.

## How it works

Nine stages. Each one names the skill it is going to give you, teaches it, works through an example,
hands you one drill to practise, and ends with a short self-check. You are meant to be able to answer
those questions before you move on. If you can't, the next stage will not land, because it is built on
the one before it.

There are no reading lists here and no links off to the library mid-lesson. The 582 book notes are one
click away and will still be there afterwards. Sending you to them halfway through a lesson would break
the thing this course is for, which is building one skill on top of another in order.

## Where it goes

It starts with what a price chart actually is — which sounds too basic to bother with, and is where
most people's mistakes are actually rooted. It ends with the matching engine: the auction that produces
every price you have been reading, and the order-flow evidence of what large participants are doing
inside it.

The middle is the part that decides whether anyone makes money. Stage 6 is where risk enters, and it is
deliberately placed *after* you can read a chart and *before* you learn a single intraday setup. Almost
every course does this the other way round, teaches entries first and money management as an appendix,
and produces traders who can spot a pattern and still lose everything.

## What it will not do

It will not give you a system to run. There is no set of parameters at the end that you can turn on.
What it gives you is the ability to read what is in front of you, size a position so that being wrong is
survivable, and judge a method — including the ones sold to you — on evidence rather than on how
confident its author sounds.

That is the skill. The rest is practice, and the practice is yours.

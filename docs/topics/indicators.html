What it is

An indicator is a transformation of price, volume or breadth data into a second series that is easier to read than the raw chart. The category divides into a few families: trend tools that smooth price (moving averages, MACD, VIDYA), oscillators that bound momentum into a fixed range (RSI, stochastics, CCI, Williams %R), volatility measures that adapt to recent dispersion (Bollinger Bands, BandWidth, Keltner channels), and breadth or flow measures built from data outside the single instrument (TICK, advance-decline lines, accumulation/distribution, volume spread analysis). The recurring lesson across the better sources is that no indicator generates an edge alone, that the popular default settings are conventions rather than optima, and that most price-derived indicators are close mathematical relatives of one another — so stacking three of them is not three pieces of evidence.

Core principles

  • Most price-based indicators are near-duplicates. Chande and Kroll report correlations of 0.77-0.93 between momentum, RSI, stochastics, CMO and ADX on the same data, so “confirmation” from a second price oscillator adds little information The New Technical Trader: Boost Your Profit by Plugging Into the Latest Indicators.
  • Confirmation should therefore come from a differently-constructed source — price, volume-and-price, and range-and-volume — rather than from a colinear cousin Using Bollinger Bands Bollinger Bands: Using Volatility The Forex Report - Core Concepts: Signal Selection.
  • Bands answer whether price is high or low relatively, not absolutely, because their width is set by recent volatility Bollinger on Bollinger Bands Bollinger Band System.
  • A band tag is not a fade signal. Price can walk the band through an entire trend, and a close outside the band is more often continuation than reversal Bollinger on Bollinger Bands John A. Bollinger, CFA, CMT — Market Master.
  • Oscillators break down in trending markets, sticking at an extreme while the trend continues — the most widely acknowledged failure mode in the category Measuring Internal Strength: Wilder's RSI Indicator Technical Indicators Tutorial The Forex Report - Core Concepts: Signal Selection.
  • Thresholds are instrument- and era-specific. Bollinger scales the standard-deviation multiplier with the averaging period, Nison says disparity zones differ per market, Thorp says a security’s MACD extremes must be charted before they are used, and Ord’s TICK levels were calibrated to 1990s NYSE breadth Bollinger on Bollinger Bands The Disparity Index The MACD: A Combo of Indicators for the Best of Both Worlds Picking Tops And Bottoms With The Tick Index.
  • Fitting a threshold to one chart after the fact is curve-fitting; Kaufman flags his own ±2.00 MACD level as exactly that MACD Crossover and Divergence System.
  • Divergence is the most widely shared signal in the category, and the most widely qualified: it warns of a possible change, and can persist far longer than expected Divergence Decisions Trading with MACD: A Lesson on Divergence Reverse Divergences and Momentum.
  • Divergence needs a separate trigger. Riordan requires the MACD to break a horizontal line drawn at its intervening extreme; Pring requires an independent trendline break or moving-average cross Trading with MACD: A Lesson on Divergence Reverse Divergences and Momentum.
  • Averages lag by construction: today’s average is plotted against today’s price, so it keeps rising briefly after price has turned New Twist on an Old Indicator The MACD: A Combo of Indicators for the Best of Both Worlds.
  • Adaptive construction is one answer to lag — scaling an EMA’s smoothing constant by a volatility or momentum measure so it speeds up in trends and flattens in quiet markets The New Technical Trader: Boost Your Profit by Plugging Into the Latest Indicators.
  • Volume and breadth carry information price alone does not: divergence between a cumulative volume-flow line and price is the basis of both Williams’s accumulation/distribution work and Volume Spread Analysis The Secret of Selecting Stocks for Immediate and Substantial Gains Undeclared Stockmarket Secrets The Wyckoff Method of Trading Stocks.
  • Higher-timeframe agreement is used across the category as a permission filter rather than an entry: trade only in the direction the slower chart allows Multi-dimensional Time Trading Moving Average Convergence-Divergence (MACD) Divergence Decisions.
  • Chart interval choice changes what a signal shows: a longer bar smooths away the swings that will actually hit the stop The Forex Report - Core Concepts: Signal Selection.
  • Almost nothing in this category is tested. Chande and Kroll, Bollinger and Morris-style parameter work are exceptions; most sources present selected charts The New Technical Trader: Boost Your Profit by Plugging Into the Latest Indicators Bollinger on Bollinger Bands.

Concrete rules and setups

Bands and volatility

  1. Bollinger Bands: 20-period simple moving average ±2 standard deviations as the default; scale the multiplier with the period — roughly 1.5σ at 10 periods, 2.0σ at 20, 2.5σ at 50 — which holds containment near 88-89% rather than the 95% a normal distribution would imply Bollinger on Bollinger Bands John A. Bollinger, CFA, CMT — Market Master.
  2. %b = (last − lower band) ÷ (upper band − lower band): 1.0 at the upper band, 0.5 at the middle, 0.0 at the lower, and unbounded beyond either Using Bollinger Bands.
  3. BandWidth = (upper − lower) ÷ middle. A Squeeze is BandWidth at a six-month low, signalling that low volatility will be followed by high volatility — a precondition, not a direction Method I — The Squeeze / Volatility Breakout Bollinger Bands: Using Volatility.
  4. Method I: after a Squeeze, buy a close above the upper band or sell short a close below the lower band; expect head fakes, and either wait for the move to develop or take half a position on the first thrust and half on confirmation Method I — The Squeeze / Volatility Breakout.
  5. Method II: buy when %b > 0.8 and the 10-period Money Flow Index > 80; mirror for shorts. Set the confirming indicator’s period to roughly half the band period Method II — Trend Following with %b and MFI.
  6. Method III: buy when %b < 0.05 while a 21-day Intraday Intensity % or 20-day AD% is above zero — a band tag not confirmed by flow; mirror for shorts Method III — Reversals.
  7. Mean-reversion band variant: buy a lower-band penetration that shows signs of turning, sell an upper-band penetration, and exit near the middle average rather than expecting a run from band to band Bollinger Band System.
  8. Squeeze via channels: Bollinger Bands (20, 2) moving inside Keltner Channels (20, 1.5) as the compression signal, with direction supplied separately Not So Squeezy.

Oscillators

  1. RSI: 14-period default; 9 for intraday sensitivity, 25 for gold, silver, crude and financials. Hayden requires at least 90 and prefers 200+ periods of history before trusting a reading Measuring Internal Strength: Wilder's RSI Indicator Trend Determination: A Quick, Accurate, and Effective Methodology.
  2. RSI range shift: in an uptrend RSI tends to hold support near 40 and meet resistance near 80; in a downtrend, support near 20 and resistance near 60. A shift from a 60/20 to an 80/40 range signals the trend has changed Trend Determination: A Quick, Accurate & Effective Methodology Trend Determination: A Quick, Accurate, and Effective Methodology.
  3. RSI failure swing: a double top above 70 followed by a break of the intervening trough is a sell signal even before price turns; the inverse applies below 30 Measuring Internal Strength: Wilder's RSI Indicator.
  4. MACD: 12- and 26-period EMAs with a 9-period signal line. Buy on a cross above the signal line, sell on the reverse, and weight crossovers occurring below zero more heavily as bullish The MACD: A Combo of Indicators for the Best of Both Worlds Moving Average Convergence-Divergence (MACD).
  5. MACD divergence trigger: mark the two divergence reference points, draw a horizontal line at the MACD’s extreme between them, and enter only when MACD breaks that line; cancel if MACD retraces past the second reference first Trading with MACD: A Lesson on Divergence.
  6. Hidden divergence: in an uptrend, the oscillator makes a lower low while price makes a higher low — read as continuation and a re-entry point, not reversal. Star uses a 5/15 EMA price oscillator Hidden Divergence.
  7. Reverse divergence: price makes a lower high while the oscillator makes a higher high — price leads the oscillator into the turn. Use raw, unsmoothed data and confirm with a trendline break Reverse Divergences and Momentum.
  8. ADX: above 25-30 indicates a trending market, below indicates chop; the same reading occurs in up- and downtrends since it measures strength only. One entry technique requires ADX at or above 30, then a pullback to a 21-period moving average ADX (Average Directional Index) Technical Indicators Tutorial.
  9. Woodie’s CCI: 14-period CCI with a 6-period Turbo CCI and ±100/±200 levels. A trend requires six or more CCI bars on one side of zero; the Zero-Line Reject needs CCI to retrace through at least ±100 before turning back with the trend; the beginner stop is one to two ticks beyond the entry bar Woodies CCI Basic Patterns and Terminology NickTrader on No-Price CCI Divergence Trading.
  10. TD Sequential: a TD Price Flip initialises; a Setup is nine consecutive closes below (buy) or above (sell) the close four bars earlier, resetting on any interruption; perfection requires bar 8, 9 or later to exceed the extremes of Setup bars 6 and 7; Countdown then accumulates thirteen bars closing beyond the high or low two bars earlier Countertrend Forex Trading with TD Sequential.

Breadth, volume and flow

  1. Accumulation/distribution: net % = (close − open) ÷ (high − low), multiplied by the day’s volume and cumulated into a running line. Buy when price makes a new low the line does not confirm; sell on the mirror Accumulation/Distribution Stock Selection System.
  2. TICK reversal setup: an intraday reading beyond −800 (stronger below −1,000) at a double bottom whose second low is within 2.5 S&P points of the first, confirmed by a bullish candle; mirror above +600 at a double top. Do not expect a turn while the day’s body exceeds two S&P points The NYSE Tick Index and Candlesticks Market Turns and Continuation Moves with the Tick Index.
  3. TICK intraday thresholds: ±500 as overbought and oversold, beyond ±1,000 as extreme, shrinking toward ±100 in a strong trend; smooth raw TICK with a 10-period average before reading direction Intraday Trading with the TICK.
  4. Volume Spread Analysis: an up bar on narrow spread and low volume is No Demand; a down bar on narrow spread and low volume is No Supply; a wide bar on very high volume with a poor close is effort without result and flags absorption; a failed new high on narrow spread and high volume is an up-thrust Undeclared Stockmarket Secrets.
  5. Advance-decline line: prior value plus advances minus declines from a fixed base; smooth ratios before reading extremes, using a 10-week average as the worked example The E-Book of Technical Market Indicators.

Adaptive and composite systems

  1. CMO and VIDYA: CMO = (Su − Sd) ÷ (Su + Sd) over nine days; VIDYA = (0.5 × |CMO|) × close + (1 − 0.5 × |CMO|) × prior VIDYA. Go long on a buy stop one tick above today’s high when today’s and yesterday’s closes are both above VIDYA, mirror for shorts, reverse on the opposite signal, with a flat $1,500 stop in the authors’ own test The CMO-Driven VIDYA Trading System.
  2. Elder-ray: Bull Power = high − 13-day EMA, Bear Power = low − 13-day EMA. Trade divergences between these histograms and price, only in the direction of the 13-day EMA’s slope Elder-ray (Bull Power / Bear Power).
  3. E.A.S.Y. method: enter long only when the Heiken Ashi close is above the Price Action Channel high average, the channel slopes up, and the RSI-based Traders Dynamic Index price line is above 50, above its trade signal line and above the market base line; exit immediately if price closes back inside the channel E.A.S.Y. Trading Method.

Common mistakes

  • Stacking several price-derived oscillators and treating their agreement as independent confirmation The New Technical Trader: Boost Your Profit by Plugging Into the Latest Indicators Using Bollinger Bands Not So Squeezy.
  • Fading every band tag, which Bollinger names as the single greatest myth about his own indicator Bollinger on Bollinger Bands.
  • Trading oscillator extremes mechanically in a trend, where RSI or stochastics can stay pinned for the whole move Measuring Internal Strength: Wilder's RSI Indicator Technical Indicators Tutorial.
  • Adopting default parameters as though they were optimal rather than conventional Bollinger Bands Revealed (Trading Lesson 31) Trend Determination: A Quick, Accurate, and Effective Methodology.
  • Choosing a threshold because it worked on the chart in front of you MACD Crossover and Divergence System.
  • Entering on the divergence itself rather than waiting for the trigger that confirms it Trading with MACD: A Lesson on Divergence Divergence Decisions.
  • Smoothing an oscillator used for divergence, which delays the turn and can invalidate the signal Reverse Divergences and Momentum.
  • Reading a squeeze or a volatility contraction as directional Bollinger Bands: Using Volatility Not So Squeezy Method I — The Squeeze / Volatility Breakout.
  • Choosing a chart interval because it makes the signal look clean, when the smoothed-away swings are what trigger the stop The Forex Report - Core Concepts: Signal Selection.
  • Applying 1990s breadth thresholds to modern markets whose composition and typical TICK magnitudes have changed Picking Tops And Bottoms With The Tick Index Market Turns and Continuation Moves with the Tick Index.
  • Running an indicator system with no stop, which most sources in this category simply omit ADX (Average Directional Index) Hidden Divergence Moving Average Convergence-Divergence (MACD).

Best books for this topic

  1. The New Technical Trader: Boost Your Profit by Plugging Into the Latest Indicators — the most quantitatively honest book here: explicit formulas, worked calculations, real backtests with trade counts and payoff ratios, and the correlation table showing why most indicators duplicate each other.
  2. Bollinger on Bollinger Bands — the creator’s own manual, with %b, BandWidth, the Squeeze and three complete rule sets, plus candid statistics on what the bands actually contain.
  3. The Secret of Selecting Stocks for Immediate and Substantial Gains — the original accumulation/distribution formula and divergence screen, fully specified and easy to code, though heavily dated in its market context.
  4. Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities, Volume 5 (1987) — worth it for the Wyckoff series and Sweeney’s Maximum Adverse Excursion technique for sizing stops from data; the rest is dated.
  5. Undeclared Stockmarket Secrets — the clearest statement of Volume Spread Analysis, for traders who want to read volume against spread rather than add another oscillator.
  6. Trend Determination: A Quick, Accurate & Effective Methodology and Trend Determination: A Quick, Accurate, and Effective Methodology — Hayden’s Cardwell-derived RSI range-shift framework, the most substantive alternative to textbook 70/30 usage.
  7. Technical Indicators Tutorial — the best single reference for conventional definitions and signal rules across the whole toolkit.
  8. Woodies CCI Basic Patterns and Terminology — the most completely specified oscillator system in the category, with entries, stops and exits actually written down.
  9. Countertrend Forex Trading with TD Sequential — a compact statement of a genuinely different construction: a bar-counting exhaustion model rather than a smoothed average.
  10. Reverse Divergences and Momentum — short, and the clearest treatment of the case where price, not the oscillator, leads the turn.

Open debates

  • What divergence means. The textbook reading treats it as a reversal warning Measuring Internal Strength: Wilder's RSI Indicator Trading with MACD: A Lesson on Divergence. Hayden inverts it, treating bearish divergence in an uptrend as a pause signal and evidence the bull trend persists Trend Determination: A Quick, Accurate & Effective Methodology. Star’s hidden divergence is a continuation signal by construction Hidden Divergence, and Pring’s reverse divergence is a third pattern again Reverse Divergences and Momentum. The same word covers four different setups.
  • Fade the band or trade the break. Bollinger’s own Method I buys strength through the upper band after a Squeeze, while Method III fades an unconfirmed band tag — and he offers no rule for reconciling the two when both fire on the same instrument Method I — The Squeeze / Volatility Breakout Method III — Reversals. Kaufman’s treatment is straightforwardly mean-reverting and concedes it fails in sustained trends Bollinger Band System.
  • Whether oscillators have any edge in raw form. Larry Williams argues in the S&C anthology that stochastics shows no obvious edge and that crossover charts only look convincing in hindsight — in the same volume that runs an uncritical explanation of the indicator Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities, Volume 5 (1987).
  • Confluence versus parsimony. One tradition stacks many tools and trades only on agreement Not So Squeezy E.A.S.Y. Trading Method TraderBO Divergence System; Chande and Kroll argue this is largely illusory because the tools measure the same thing, and prefer a few genuinely different constructions The New Technical Trader: Boost Your Profit by Plugging Into the Latest Indicators.
  • Whether lag can be removed. Adaptive averages address it by varying the smoothing constant from data already in hand The New Technical Trader: Boost Your Profit by Plugging Into the Latest Indicators; the neural-network approach claims to predict a future average from intermarket data, on evidence of a four-trade sample, and the displaced average offered as a do-it-yourself substitute is flagged by its own author as needing more research New Twist on an Old Indicator.
  • Whether price can be dropped entirely. The Woodie’s CCI room argues divergence patterns can be read from the oscillator alone with price hidden; participants in the same transcript dispute it NickTrader on No-Price CCI Divergence Trading Woodies CCI Basic Patterns and Terminology.
  • Mechanical versus discretionary volume reading. Williams’s A/D formula turns volume into a computable line Accumulation/Distribution Stock Selection System, while VSA and Wyckoff leave “high” and “low” volume to visual judgement, which makes them unreproducible but arguably more adaptable Undeclared Stockmarket Secrets The Wyckoff Method of Trading Stocks.

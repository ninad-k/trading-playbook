Summary

A slide-deck seminar by Greg Capra of Pristine.com (co-author of “Tools and Tactics for the Master Day Trader”) teaching a discretionary intraday methodology for active stocks. It frames four trading styles (core, swing, guerrilla, day) by holding period, then builds a “foundation” of simplified candlestick reading, 20- and 200-period simple moving averages on 5-/15-/60-minute charts, percentage retracement zones, and the proprietary “Pristine Buy & Sell Setup” pattern, combined with the TICK and TRIN broad-market breadth indicators to time entries.

Key points

  • Four trading styles by holding period: core (weekly charts, weeks-to-months), swing (daily charts, days-to-weeks), guerrilla (daily/60-/30-min, hours-to-days), day (5-/15-/60-min, minutes-to-hours).
  • Day trading requires confidence, patience, and discipline, built through a systematic, objective (non-subjective) method of chart interpretation.
  • Core tools: 5-, 15-, and 60-minute candlestick charts with color-coded volume, plus 20-period and 200-period simple moving averages.
  • Six simplified candle concepts flag a probable trend change or high-probability trade: Bottoming Tail (BT), Topping Tail (TT), Narrowing Range Bodies (NRB), Narrow Range Body (NR), Wide Range Body (WRB), and “Changing of the Guard” (COG).
  • The “Pristine Buy & Sell Setup” is a multi-bar reversal pattern usable on any timeframe, considered complete once a reversal candle forms.
  • Retracement zones and their trend implication: 40% retracement = trend still positive; 50% = positive-to-neutral; 60% = neutral-to-negative; 80-100% = trend considered negative; over 100% = support/resistance is taken out and the prior trend is negated.
  • TICK and TRIN (broad-market breadth indicators) are used alongside price structure: TRIN above 1.0 and rising signals sellers in control, TRIN above 1.0 but falling signals sellers losing control; TICK reaching a broad-market support/resistance extreme is used to time reversals.
  • A worked “putting it together” example: start with the longer timeframe (e.g., 60-min) to identify a WRB/moving-average divergence setup near prior support, then drop to the 5-minute chart to time entry off a COG or TT/BT candle combined with TICK/TRIN confirmation.

Actionable rules

  1. Use 20-period and 200-period simple moving averages on 5-, 15-, and 60-minute candlestick charts as the core technical toolkit — no other indicators specified.
  2. Treat a retracement beyond 60% (and especially 80-100%) of the prior move as a warning that the trend may be turning negative; a retracement over 100% negates the prior support/resistance level.
  3. Confirm intraday entries by starting from a longer timeframe (60-min) for context, then timing the actual entry on a shorter timeframe (5-min) using a candle-based reversal signal (COG, TT, or BT) at a support/resistance level flagged by the longer timeframe.
  4. Use TRIN direction (rising vs. falling above 1.0) as a real-time read on whether sellers or buyers are gaining control of the broad market before entering.
  5. Target the 200-period MA as a resistance/objective level after a deep retracement above the 20-period MA followed by a Pristine Buy Setup.

Caveats

This is a marketing/seminar deck for Pristine.com’s paid courses and “Tools and Tactics for the Master Day Trader” book, so several proprietary terms (Pristine Buy & Sell Setup, Changing of the Guard, guerrilla trading) are trademarked shorthand introduced without full technical specification — exact entry/stop rules for the Pristine Setup are not spelled out beyond “setup complete after a reversal candle forms.” Includes standard hypothetical-performance risk disclaimers; no backtested statistics are given. Content assumes access to Pristine’s own charting conventions (color-coded volume, candle nomenclature).

Who it is for

Beginner-to-intermediate day traders wanting a structured, candlestick-plus-moving-average framework for reading intraday charts and multi-timeframe entries, understanding it is a promotional preview of a larger paid course.

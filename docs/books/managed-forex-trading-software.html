Summary

A short opinion piece by Winsor A.G.A. Hoang, founder of a managed-forex signal/software service, arguing that marketing claims about trading forex profitably in “15 minutes a day” are unrealistic since forex is a 24-hour market that professional full-time traders monitor 10-12 hours daily. The main content is a set of due-diligence questions to ask before buying a forex course, book, or system: whether the seller earns income from trading or from selling the product, how long a genuine mentor believes it takes to become competent (compared to multi-year medical/professional apprenticeships), and skepticism toward cheaply sold “expert advisors” that claim large returns. It closes by promoting the author’s own managed-account/signal service as the solution for people who cannot trade full-time.

Key points

  • Forex is a continuous 24-hour market; the article argues no one can meaningfully trade it well in 15 minutes a day.
  • Vetting question for any seller: “Is the author making money from trading or from the profits of selling the item?”
  • Vetting question for a paid mentor: how long would it take to become good under their mentorship — a “few months” answer is a red flag.
  • Analogy to licensed professions: doctors need two years of residency, many trades need 12-18 month apprenticeships.
  • Red flag on cheap expert advisors: if a $300-$1,200 EA genuinely made $1,000/month, why sell it that cheaply?
  • Recommends six-to-nine-month live track records over forum testimonials or short performance claims.
  • The article’s own proposed solution is renting a managed forex trading software service from a “creditable and trusted” vendor.

Actionable rules

  1. Before buying a forex course, book, or system, ask whether the seller’s primary income is trading or selling the product.
  2. Before hiring a paid mentor, ask how long they estimate it would take you to become profitable; be skeptical of a “few months” answer.
  3. Be skeptical of cheap expert advisors claiming large monthly returns.
  4. Require six-to-nine-month verified live track records, not short samples or testimonials.
  5. If unable to trade full-time, consider a reputable managed account/signal service (the article’s own recommended path).

Caveats

A short promotional article from the founder of a managed-forex-signal business, whose closing recommendation directly promotes his own product — a conflict of interest against the article’s own vetting test. No trading rules, indicators, or system content.

Who it is for

Beginners evaluating whether to buy a forex course, EA, or mentorship program, wanting basic due-diligence questions rather than a trading technique.

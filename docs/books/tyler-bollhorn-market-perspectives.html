Summary

A compilation of Stockscores.com educational strategy articles by Tyler Bollhorn, each structured around a concept, screening criteria (built for the Stockscores Market Scan tool), and a visual/chart-based confirmation step. The pieces covered include “Bottom Fishing” (buying reversing downtrends), a support-and-resistance primer, “Profiting from Euphoria” (shorting/exiting overextended spike moves), and an “Adoption Cycle of Stocks” essay mapping a stock’s psychological life cycle from consolidation through euphoric top to renewed base.

Key points

  • Bottom Fishing defines three reversal stages: a break from a declining trendline (pessimism), a rising-bottom consolidation above the prior low (confidence), and a breakout from that rising bottom (optimism); watch-list stages 1-2, or wait for stage 3 confirmation.
  • Bottom Fishing scan: bullish candle, bullish short-term MA but bearish medium-term MA, price ≥25% below its 150-day high, bullish volume-accumulation indicators, minimum $250,000 dollar-volume.
  • Support is a price floor strengthened by repeated tests; resistance is a ceiling strengthened the same way; both cluster at round dollar values as psychological anchors.
  • A resistance breakout on above-average volume tends to continue; failure to hold above a broken level warns the move may fail.
  • “Profiting from Euphoria” targets stocks up ≥25% in 30 days, volume above the 150-day average, RSI overbought, high/rising volatility, and a bearish (red) candle — exhaustion of an emotion-driven spike.
  • Both strategies warn they are not 100% effective and instruct taking losses early if the setup is invalidated.
  • The Adoption Cycle essay maps a repeatable cycle: consolidation, breakout on fundamental change, a shallow pullback, a mid-trend profit-taking pullback, a euphoric blow-off top, a “sucker rally” failing to make new highs, then breakdown as late holders still cite “the fundamentals.”

Actionable rules

  1. Bottom Fishing entry: stock ≥25% below its 150-day high, bullish short-term MA against a still-bearish medium-term MA, bullish volume-accumulation, $250,000+ daily dollar volume.
  2. Bottom Fishing exit: caution or exit if price falls back below the resistance level it just broke.
  3. Profiting from Euphoria entry: ≥25% gain in 30 days, volume above the 150-day average, RSI overbought, high/rising 20-day volatility, bearish candle making a new intraday high before closing down.
  4. Profiting from Euphoria invalidation: skip or exit if the bearish candle is followed by a bullish day, or it failed to print a new high.
  5. No universal position-sizing or stop-percentage figures are given; risk control is “take your loss and move on” when the pattern is invalidated.

Caveats

The scan criteria are built for the proprietary Stockscores.com Market Scan tool and its named indicators, which readers without that platform must replicate manually. Published in 2000, so price/volume thresholds reflect that era’s markets; no formal backtest statistics are given for either strategy.

Who it is for

Swing traders comfortable with chart-based pattern reading and volume/momentum screening who want concrete, filter-based setups for both reversal entries (bottom fishing) and fading momentum exhaustion (euphoria fade).

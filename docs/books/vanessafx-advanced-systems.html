VanessaFX Advanced Systems

Vanessa FX and Nick B · 2006 · Tier B — concise note manual intermediate

A discretionary forex e-book pairing a 4-hour swing system (GBP/USD, GBP/JPY) with a 30-minute intraday system (USD/CHF, EUR/USD), both built on manually-drawn support/resistance plus trend indicators.

Summary

A paid e-book (sold with a private forum for follow-up support) presenting two related trading systems, both anchored on manually-plotted support and resistance lines the trader redraws from recent price history. The author is explicit that the technical rules only signal entries — exits and fine-tuning are described as instinct-based skills developed through experience, illustrated with a series of annotated live-trade examples rather than a fully mechanical rule set.

Key points

  • Chart setup: six charts total — USD/CHF 30-min, EUR/USD 30-min, GBP/USD 4hr + Daily, GBP/JPY 4hr + Daily; the author insists on using only these, rejecting the common habit of running many charts and systems at once.
  • Support/resistance method: each morning, identify areas price has repeatedly bounced from on the 30-min/4hr charts and draw a line; also plot yearly, monthly, weekly, and previous-day high/low zones (daily lines reset each day). Pivot points are explicitly rejected as a substitute.
  • 4hr System indicators: 6 EMA, 13 SMA, MACD(12,26,9), Parabolic SAR(0.02, 0.2), plus 120 SMA and 300 SMA (replacing an earlier 100/200 SMA version after 5 years of backtesting on the author’s simulator); daily chart carries 20/50/100/200 SMAs for added context.
  • 4hr System entry: long when the 6 EMA crosses above the 13 SMA and at least one of MACD-cross or Parabolic SAR flip also confirms an uptrend (reverse for shorts) — only 2 of the 3 confirming signals are required, not all 3.
  • Intraday System indicators: 30-min USD/CHF and EUR/USD charts with Awesome Oscillator and Parabolic SAR(0.02, 0.2) only.
  • Intraday System entry: long when Parabolic SAR flips to the bottom of the candle and the Awesome Oscillator turns green (reverse, SAR to top plus red Oscillator, for shorts).
  • The 120/300 SMA band on the 4hr chart is used as dynamic support/resistance; price trapped between the two SMAs is called “no man’s land,” where the author often reduces size or avoids trading, tightening targets and stops if a trade is taken.
  • A break of the daily 100 SMA or 200 SMA is treated as a strong continuation signal — a 200 SMA break implies a stronger, longer rally than a 100 SMA break.

Actionable rules

  1. 4hr stop loss: 100 pips initial stop on GBP/USD, 150 pips on GBP/JPY; move stop to breakeven once up 100 pips (GBP/USD) or 150 pips (GBP/JPY), then trail (e.g., lock in 100 pips once up 200 pips).
  2. Intraday stop/target: USD/CHF — 25 pip stop, 30 pip target; EUR/USD — 30 pip stop, 40 pip target.
  3. Risk/reward preference: author targets at least a 1:2 risk/reward ratio when placing discretionary stops (e.g., a 60-pip stop against a 100+ pip expected move).
  4. Stop placement convention: always set stops on odd numbers, avoiding those ending in 9 or 1, with at least 5 pips of breathing room beyond the relevant support/resistance line.
  5. Exit discretion: exit fully if price touches a support/resistance line and reverses by the next candle; exit only half the position if the line is merely tested without a clear reversal candle.
  6. Trend filter: never trade against the 120/300 SMA band context on the 4hr system without waiting for a confirmed break.
  7. Leverage cap: never trade above 100:1 leverage on these systems.

Caveats

Entries are explicitly hybrid — mechanical indicator alignment for triggers, but discretionary judgment (described as “instinct” gained through experience) for stop placement, position sizing on exits, and trade management, so the system cannot be fully mechanized from the text alone. No formal backtest statistics (win rate, drawdown, sample size) are provided; support comes from a curated set of annotated live-trade screenshots, and the 120/300 SMA parameters are said to be based on “5 years of chart data” tested on the author’s own simulator without disclosed methodology. Written for and marketed to a 2006-era retail forex audience with leverage and broker assumptions (e.g., 100:1+ leverage readily available) that may not match current regulatory environments.

Who it is for

Discretionary swing and intraday forex traders comfortable combining a small set of moving averages, MACD, Parabolic SAR, and the Awesome Oscillator with manually-drawn support/resistance, and who are willing to develop judgment-based exit skills rather than follow a fully mechanical rule set.

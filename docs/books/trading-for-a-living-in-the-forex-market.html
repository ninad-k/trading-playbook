Summary

A generalist forex training manual (Trading International LLC, 2004) organized as a five-part course: (1) Forex market structure, sectors (spot/forward/futures/options), and risks; (2) major currencies and trading systems (broker vs. direct dealing); (3) fundamental analysis, including exchange-rate theories (purchasing power parity, elasticities) and a full catalog of U.S. economic indicators; (4) technical analysis — Dow theory, chart types, trendlines, reversal and continuation patterns, gaps, and a survey of indicators (moving averages, Bollinger Bands, ATR, CCI, DMI, stochastics, MACD, RSI, ROC, Williams %R, Ichimoku); and (5) Fibonacci ratios and Elliott wave theory. It functions as a reference primer rather than a tradable system.

Key points

  • Frames Forex as distinct from other markets due to sensitivity to macro/political events, 24-hour access, high liquidity, and low barriers to entry.
  • Covers the four main Forex sectors: spot, forward, futures, and currency options.
  • Surveys fundamental drivers: GNP/GDP, industrial production, inflation (PPI/CPI), trade balance, employment data, and consumer spending indicators, plus financial/sociopolitical influences.
  • Explains Dow Theory basics and the standard reversal patterns (head-and-shoulders, double/triple tops and bottoms, rounding patterns) and continuation patterns (flags, pennants, triangles, wedges, rectangles).
  • Classifies gap types: common, breakaway, runaway, and exhaustion gaps.
  • Surveys a full indicator toolkit (moving averages, envelopes, Bollinger Bands, ATR, oscillators, MACD, RSI, ROC, Williams %R, Ichimoku) without giving specific parameter recommendations.
  • Introduces Fibonacci retracement levels and Elliott wave structure as a closing topic, with a brief note on placing stops slightly beyond a 61.8% retracement level.

Actionable rules

None given with specific numeric parameters — the manual explains concepts (patterns, indicators, Fibonacci levels) generically rather than prescribing entries, exits, or position sizing. The single concrete mechanic offered is placing a protective stop just beyond a 61.8% Fibonacci retracement of the prior impulse wave when trading a corrective bounce.

Caveats

This is educational background material, not a tested trading system — it defines terms and concepts across fundamental and technical analysis without backtest data, risk parameters, or a stated edge. Content is standard and dated in places (e.g., discussion of direct-dealing vs. broker trade systems reflects mid-2000s retail forex market structure).

Who it is for

Complete beginners who need a single-document overview of forex market structure, fundamental drivers, and the standard technical-analysis toolkit before moving to a book with an actual tested system.

Summary

The supplied 12-page extract is a table of contents, not the textbook’s instructional chapters. It maps an eight-part fixed-income text: investment instruments; term-structure theory and curve construction; interest-rate hedging; passive and active portfolio management; swaps, forwards, and futures; term-structure and credit-spread models; vanilla and exotic derivatives; and securitization (pp. 1-11). The contents show where subjects occur in the full book, but they contain no definitions, formulas, worked examples, empirical results, or trading rules. Page 12 contains no extracted substantive text.

Key points

  • Part I lists bonds, money-market instruments, bond pricing, discounting, compounding conventions, yield to maturity, spot rates, forward rates, and par yield (pp. 1-2).
  • Part II covers empirical yield-curve properties, classical term-structure theories, Treasury and interbank zero-coupon curves, interpolation, least-squares methods, and credit-spread curves (pp. 2-3).
  • Part III lists duration, modified duration, convexity, principal components, and three-factor yield-curve hedging (pp. 3-4).
  • Part IV separates passive replication and tracking-error methods from active interest-rate, relative-value, spread, and convergence strategies, then adds performance measurement (pp. 4-5).
  • Parts V and VI cover swaps, forwards, futures, clearing and margin, cheapest-to-deliver analysis, yield-curve models, and credit-spread dynamics (pp. 5-7).
  • Parts VII and VIII list embedded bond options, convertibles, caps, floors, swaptions, exotic options, credit derivatives, mortgage-backed securities, and asset-backed securities (pp. 8-11).

Actionable rules

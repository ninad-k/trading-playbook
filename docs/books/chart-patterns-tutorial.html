Summary

A general technical-analysis primer republished on ActionForex.com and credited to TradingEducation.com, covering the standard toolkit of price-chart analysis: chart types (close-only, bar, candlestick), trend line and channel construction, continuation patterns, reversal patterns, support/resistance identification, retracement levels, and price gaps. It is definitional/educational rather than a specific tradable system — it names patterns and their typical implications rather than giving numeric entry/exit rules.

Key points

  • Uptrend = series of higher highs and higher lows; downtrend = series of lower highs and lower lows; a trend line’s validity increases with the number of times price touches and respects it.
  • Continuation patterns covered: bullish/bearish flags and pennants (flagpole length can project a price target), symmetrical/ascending/descending triangles (breakout expected in the direction of the prior dominant trend), and cup-and-saucer (cup-and-handle) bottoms.
  • Reversal patterns covered: double/triple tops and bottoms (confirmed only once price breaks the interim high/low between the two peaks/troughs), head-and-shoulders top/bottom (neckline break confirms the pattern; head-to-neckline distance projects a price target), rising/falling wedges, and the rare diamond top pattern.
  • Support/resistance can come from trend lines, prior price clusters, unfilled gaps, moving averages, Gann angles, or round “psychological” price levels; a broken support level tends to become new resistance and vice versa.
  • Popular retracement levels: 50% (most cited), 33%/67%, and Fibonacci 38.2%/61.8%; a retracement gains validity when it coincides with another support/resistance signal.
  • Gaps: a gap-up bar’s low exceeds the prior bar’s high (or vice versa for gap-down); breakaway gaps signal a new trend/breakout, continuation gaps confirm an accelerating trend, and exhaustion gaps appear near a market’s final high or low before reversal.
  • The article stresses combining technical analysis (price-based) with awareness of fundamentals (news, economic reports), even for a primarily technical trader.

Actionable rules

None given as a numbered, backtested system — the document defines patterns and their typical implications rather than prescribing specific entries, stops, or position sizes. The closest actionable guidance: treat a double top/bottom as confirmed only when price breaks the interim low/high between the two peaks/troughs; treat a trend line break as valid reversal evidence only with next-session follow-through (unless the penetration is large enough to negate the line outright).

Caveats

Undated attribution (TradingEducation.com via ActionForex, page timestamp reads 2006) with no named individual author; content is generic pattern-recognition material found in most classical technical-analysis references and is not backtested or quantified with win rates in this document. All chart examples are attributed to “VantagePoint Intermarket Analysis Software,” a specific vendor’s charting package, though no vendor-specific claims are made in the text itself.

Who it is for

Beginners who need a single-document reference for the vocabulary and shapes of classical chart patterns before studying a specific tradable system built on them.

#1 Forex Trading Course

David C. Arena · Tier B — concise note course beginner

Retail forex course covering pip/lot mechanics, order types, and a candlestick-plus-MACD entry strategy, wrapped around risk-management rules of thumb and an affiliate-marketing pitch for the author's broker.

Summary

A self-published retail forex course whose title promises turning $1,260 into $12,300 in 30 days, but whose actual content is a standard beginner curriculum: forex/interbank market mechanics, pip and lot-size calculations, leverage and margin, order types (market, stop, trailing stop, OCO), basic trend/trendline analysis, and a strategy section combining candlestick reversal patterns with MACD signals. The back third reproduces detailed MACD and MACD-Histogram explanations (matching material found elsewhere in this library, e.g. StockCharts-style writeups) and closes with broker recommendation and an affiliate-income pitch.

Key points

  • Pip value calculation — worked examples for USD/JPY, USD/CHF, USD/CAD (pip = 0.01 or 0.0001 divided by the exchange rate) and for pairs quoted USD-second (EUR/USD, GBP/USD), which need an extra conversion step back to USD.
  • Standard lot sizing — $100,000 standard lot, $10,000 mini lot; pip value scales with lot size (e.g., ~$8.56/pip for a $100,000 USD/JPY position near 116.73).
  • Order types — market, stop, trailing stop, and One-Cancels-Other (OCO) orders, each illustrated with a worked entry/exit example in pips and dollars.
  • Trend basics — markets move Bullish, Bearish, or Neutral/sideways; an uptrendline needs at least two rising lows, a downtrendline at least two falling highs.
  • Candlestick + MACD strategy — the course’s core method: identify a candlestick reversal pattern (bullish or bearish), then look for MACD confirmation (moving-average crossover or centerline crossover) in the same direction before entering; more agreeing signals increase confidence but are not required.
  • MACD/MACD-Histogram mechanics — detailed coverage of the 12/26-day EMA construction, 9-day signal line, bullish/bearish moving-average and centerline crossovers, and using the Histogram to anticipate MACD crossovers via divergence.
  • Order ticket workflow — a step-by-step checklist for placing a live trade (account number, order type, symbol, lot size, time-in-force, quote verification).

Actionable rules

  1. Use no more than 10% of account equity as margin at any given time (stated as a rule of thumb).
  2. Cap the stop-loss at 25% of the initial deposit on any single trade (worked example: $1,012.60 deposit, 25% = $253.15 max loss, translating to roughly a 25-pip stop at the example rate).
  3. Set a stop-loss immediately upon entering every position; move to a trailing stop once the trade moves in your favor, tightening it in fixed pip increments (10 pips per example step).
  4. Enter only when a candlestick reversal signal aligns with a MACD crossover (moving-average or centerline) in the same direction; treat additional agreeing signals as higher-confidence, not mandatory.
  5. Use an OCO order to bracket a breakout level (e.g., buy-stop above resistance, sell-stop below support) so only the triggered side remains live.

Caveats

The “$1,260 to $12,300 in 30 days” title is not supported anywhere in the body — no such trade log, backtest, or return claim appears in the readable content, and the course otherwise reads as generic retail forex education. Large sections (roughly pages 24–86) are chart/diagram pages with little to no extractable text and could not be reviewed. The MACD/candlestick material closely mirrors widely circulated technical-analysis writeups rather than original research, and the course’s final chapters are a pitch to sell the reader an “affiliate business” reselling the same course and a named broker, which is a conflict of interest for any strategy claims made earlier.

Who it is for

Complete beginners wanting a single walkthrough of forex order mechanics and a simple candlestick + MACD confirmation strategy, provided they ignore the return claims in the title and the affiliate-marketing chapters at the end.

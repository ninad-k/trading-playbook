Summary

This 13-page extract is the front matter of Joe Ross’s Trading Manual: Tips, Tricks, Strategies, and Tactics for Traders. It contains the 2002 copyright and publisher details, a table of contents, a course caution, a risk disclaimer, a short biography, and a one-page introduction (pp. 2, 5-12). The introduction says the full manual presents charts, unusual uses of indicators, ways to use news, government reports and fundamentals, and trading ideas drawn from Ross’s experience and from other successful traders (p. 12). The extract itself does not teach a setup, trading plan, recordkeeping method, stop placement, or position-sizing rule.

Key points

  • The contents list 30 chapters across two parts, followed by appendices on the Law of Charts, the Trader’s Trick, and identifying congestion (pp. 5-7).
  • Named chapter topics include money management, the ultimate stop loss, rhythm trading, day trading versus position trading, staying with a trend, divergence decisions, market manipulation, and liquidation (pp. 5-7).
  • The course caution says the manual is meant to be studied and that some sections may be difficult to understand on a first reading (p. 8).
  • The disclaimer states that the methods are not guaranteed to make profits or avoid losses and that each trader is responsible for his or her own actions (p. 9).
  • The biography says Ross began trading commodity futures in 1957, moved into day trading S&P 500 futures in 1982, and formed Trading Educators in 1988 (p. 11).
  • The introduction describes the work as a collection of tips, techniques, charts, indicator uses, fundamental inputs, and experienced trading ideas; it provides no operational detail in this extract (p. 12).

Actionable rules

  1. Use the contents pages to locate the relevant chapter before relying on a topic named in the manual (pp. 5-7).
  2. Treat the material as study material rather than a quick-reference signal sheet; that is the source’s explicit caution (p. 8).
  3. Do not infer a stop distance, timeframe, entry rule, or position percentage from these pages; none is established in the extract.

Caveats

Most of the extract is administrative front matter, and several pages contain little or no substantive text. The chapter titles show the full manual’s scope but do not supply the rules within those chapters. The disclaimer is explicit that losses can occur and that no profit claim is made (p. 9).

Who it is for

Readers deciding where to begin in the full Joe Ross manual or verifying its edition and chapter structure.

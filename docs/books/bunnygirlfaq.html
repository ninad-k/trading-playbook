BunnyGirl's FAQ

Summary

A Q&A-format FAQ (Rev 1.0, dated 28/08/04) documenting the discretionary intraday forex system of a trader known as “BunnyGirl,” trading EUR-USD, GBP-USD, and USD-CHF (occasionally EUR-JPY) on 30-minute charts. The core signal is a 5-period WMA crossing a 20-period WMA, filtered by a minimum pip move and the daily (00:00 GMT) opening price, combined with a 4-lot scale-out exit plan and several named discretionary patterns (“Mr Sheen,” “Gimme bars,” “the 5:10”).

Key points

  • Core signal: 5 WMA crosses 20 WMA on the 30-minute chart, confirmed by 100 WMA context, RSI(14), two stochastics (5,3,3 and 50,3,3), and Bollinger Bands (20,2).
  • Entry filter: price must move past the cross by 20 pips (EUR pairs) or 25 pips (others), plus spread if long, to reduce whipsaws.
  • Directional filter: only take long crosses above the day’s 00:00 GMT open, short crosses below it; avoid crosses too close to the daily open.
  • Exit plan across 4 lots: 1st out at +30 pips; 2nd out at +50 pips; 3rd trailed by the prior bar’s extreme; runner trailed by 10 pips once past +100 pips.
  • Stop management: move to breakeven at +20 pips (+10 if stalling); avoid entering/exiting in the last 5 minutes of a 30-minute bar.
  • Targets: 1st target nearest Bollinger Band, 2nd target 100 pips from the swing extreme, 3rd target the daily 20-period WMA.
  • Goal: roughly 50 pips/day, 250 pips/week; best windows 06:00–09:00 and 12:30–15:00 GMT, avoiding 10:00–12:30 and after 16:00 GMT.
  • Named setups: “Mr Sheen” (post-spike bounce off the 20/100 WMA), “Gimme bars” (range bounces), “the 5:10” (London close stop-hunts).

Actionable rules

  1. Enter on a 5/20 WMA cross on the 30-min chart once price clears the cross by 20 pips (EUR pairs) or 25 pips (other pairs), plus spread if long.
  2. Only take crosses in the direction implied by the 00:00 GMT daily open; avoid crosses within roughly 50 pips of the open.
  3. Stop-loss at the cross level (adjusted by judgment near the daily open or 100 WMA).
  4. Move stop to breakeven at +20 pips (+10 if stalling); scale out 4 lots at +30, +50, +100 pips, then trail every 10 pips.
  5. Do not enter or exit in the final 5 minutes of a 30-minute bar; wait for the next bar’s breakout of the prior bar’s high/low.

Caveats

Informal, discretionary trader notes relying heavily on subjective judgment rather than a fully mechanical, backtested system; no win rate, drawdown, or sample size supports the stated 50-pips/day target.

Who it is for

Discretionary short-term forex traders comfortable combining a simple moving-average-cross signal with multi-timeframe indicator confirmation and disciplined, staged profit-taking.

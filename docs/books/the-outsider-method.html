The Outsider Method

X-Trader · Tier B — concise note manual beginner

Two-page GBP/USD 15-minute EMA-distance breakout method with a fixed multiple-of-candle-body profit target and breakeven-plus-spread management.

Summary

A two-page trading note by an author going by “X-Trader” describing a single, fully mechanical pattern for the GBP/USD 15-minute chart. The method looks for a candle whose body and shadows stay clear of a 9-period EMA and whose close breaks the prior candle’s high or low, then enters on the next candle’s open in the direction of the break. The entire method — pattern, entry, stop, and target — fits on two pages with no discussion of psychology, broader risk management, or backtested statistics.

Key points

  • Indicator: single 9-period EMA plotted on a 15-minute GBP/USD chart.
  • Pattern filter: the signal candle’s body and shadows must not touch the EMA, ideally staying at least 1 pip clear of it.
  • Directional trigger: close above the previous candle’s high (bullish) or below the previous candle’s low (bearish).
  • Entry is always at the open of the candle immediately following the signal candle, in the direction of the trigger.
  • Stop loss and profit target are placed simultaneously as a one-cancels-the-other (OCO) pair as soon as the trade is opened.
  • Profit target is a multiple (“n”) of the signal candle’s body size (open-to-close distance), added to (bullish) or subtracted from (bearish) the entry price; the author suggests n = 2 as a starting point and notes n can be fixed or volatility-adjusted.
  • Once a trade shows 20 points (pips) or more of open profit, the stop is moved to breakeven plus the spread.

Actionable rules

  1. Plot a 9-period EMA on a GBP/USD 15-minute chart.
  2. Watch for a candle whose entire range (body and shadows) stays clear of the EMA and whose close breaks the prior candle’s high (long) or low (short).
  3. Enter at the open of the next candle in the breakout direction.
  4. Set stop loss at the low (long) or high (short) of the signal candle, or at the nearest recent swing low/high if the signal candle’s extreme is too close to entry.
  5. Set profit target at n times the signal candle’s body size from entry (n = 2 suggested starting point).
  6. Once profit reaches 20+ pips, move the stop to breakeven plus spread.

Caveats

Extremely short document with no backtest results, win-rate data, or discussion of which market sessions or conditions the pattern works best in; the “n = 2” target multiplier is presented as the author’s informal suggestion rather than an optimized parameter. No position-sizing or per-trade risk percentage is given, so it must be paired with an external money-management rule before live use.

Who it is for

Discretionary forex traders who want a compact, easy-to-code breakout pattern for a single pair and timeframe, and who already have their own position-sizing rules to layer on top.

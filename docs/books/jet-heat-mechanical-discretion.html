Summary

A self-published, informally written forex trading guide by a trader identified only as “J. Heat.” The core idea, “Mechanical Discretion” (MD), is starting from a mechanical trading system and then applying personal discretionary judgment to entries and exits, on the theory that no two traders execute the same mechanical system identically because of individual psychology (“diff factors”). To ground MD, the author presents his base system, the Jetset Trading Method (JTM): a three-timeframe chart setup borrowed from Alexander Elder’s Triple Screen, combined with three sub-strategies — Crossout, Boll Breakers, and Stochastic Divergence.

Key points

  • Triple Screen setup — three charts per pair: Day, 1 Hour, and 5 Minute, all candlestick.
  • Day chart — 100-period EMA plus trendlines, Fib projections, and support/resistance for trend direction.
  • 1 Hour chart — 5, 13, and 100-period EMAs (5/13 credited to Rob Booker).
  • 5 Minute chart — Bollinger Bands (20, 2 deviations), RSI (14, 70/30), Stochastic (15,3,3, 80/20), yesterday’s high/low, a daily pivot, and Camarilla pivots.
  • Crossout (1 Hour) — enter on a break of the prior candle’s high/low by 1-2 pips in the direction of an EMA cross, targeting the daily EMA 100.
  • Boll Breakers (5 min) — traded around Bollinger Band and Camarilla pivot touches, with separate stops for aggressive vs. non-aggressive traders.
  • Stochastic Divergence (5 min) — divergence signals confirmed against Bollinger Bands or the other two strategies.
  • “Beginner’s Trap”: abandoning a system after a normal losing streak (8-9 losses can occur) instead of evaluating a full sample period.

Actionable rules

  1. Crossout entry: buy/sell 2 pips beyond the prior candle’s high/low within 2 bars of an EMA cross, on the 1 Hour chart.
  2. Crossout stop: 35 pips from entry; move to breakeven after 10 pips profit (7 if slow-moving).
  3. Boll Breakers (aggressive): stop 10-15 pips beyond the touch high/low, or exit at middle Bollinger Band if unstopped.
  4. Boll Breakers (non-aggressive): stop 2 pips beyond the touch high/low; breakeven after 7-10 pips against-trend.
  5. Stochastic Divergence: stop 15-20 pips from entry.
  6. Only take 5-minute setups in the direction of the 1 Hour Crossout trend; prefer the higher timeframe when signals disagree.
  7. Do not abandon the system after a short losing streak.

Caveats

Self-published in an informal, unedited style with no table of contents by design; some rules rely on figures not captured in extracted text. The MD concept itself is not testable — it explicitly invites overriding mechanical rules with judgment, making performance unverifiable.

Who it is for

Discretionary forex day traders comfortable combining a multi-timeframe Triple-Screen base with subjective judgment, not traders wanting a fully rules-based system.

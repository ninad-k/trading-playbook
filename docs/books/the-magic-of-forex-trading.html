The Magic of Forex Trading: Money Management

James de Wet · Tier B — concise note manual beginner

Summary

A short promotional e-book for the Forex618.net signal service, framed around three “keys” to converting a trading account into a larger one: managing risk per trade, managing risk per account (leverage), and compounding profits. It walks through two illustrative account-growth spreadsheets — a conservative scenario (200 pips/month, no losing months) and a more realistic one (300 pips/month with three drawdown months of -350 pips) — both starting from $10,000 at 5:1 leverage, before pitching a subscription to the author’s signal service.

Key points

  • Key 1, risk per trade: close losing trades for a small loss rather than holding or adding to them; the author’s systems reportedly win 65-70% of trades.
  • Author’s stated stop-loss discipline: do not risk more than a 30-50 pip stop per trade, and never widen a stop or add to a losing trade.
  • Key 2, risk per account: cap leverage at 5:1 (5 mini lots per $10,000, or 5 standard lots per $100,000), regardless of what a broker offers (100:1-200:1).
  • Key 3, compounding: reinvesting monthly profits back into position size compounds growth; the conservative scenario grows $10,000 to $88,200 over 24 months, and the “realistic” scenario (accounting for losing months) grows it to $95,000.
  • The realistic scenario used 300 pips per winning month and three losing months of -350 pips each over 24 months, at constant 5:1 leverage.
  • The author claims verifiable broker-statement track record and a money-back guarantee for the paid signal subscription (unverifiable from the document itself).

Actionable rules

  1. Do not exceed roughly a 30-50 pip stop-loss per trade.
  2. Never add to a losing position or move a stop against yourself.
  3. Cap account leverage at about 5:1 (position size = 5x the account’s base-currency-equivalent in mini/standard lots per $10,000/$100,000), well below broker maximums.
  4. Reinvest (compound) realized profits into position size each month rather than withdrawing them, to accelerate account growth.
  5. Plan for periodic losing months (the model uses roughly 1 losing month in 8) rather than assuming a smooth equity curve.

Caveats

This is marketing material for a paid Forex signal subscription; the account-growth tables are hypothetical projections, not audited results, and the claimed 65-70% win rate and “verifiable” performance cannot be checked from the document. Spread/commission costs are only partially addressed. Treat the specific pip and dollar figures as illustrative of a compounding/leverage concept, not as a validated system.

Who it is for

Beginner Forex traders who want a simple, numeric illustration of why capped leverage and compounding matter more than chasing a “perfect” system; should be read skeptically given its promotional intent.

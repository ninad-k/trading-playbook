Summary

A beginner-oriented promotional course teaching a specific chart setup and three related day-trading approaches for the retail spot-forex market, aimed at traders using 1, 5, or 10-minute charts. It walks through choosing a broker, opening a demo account, building a standard indicator stack, and reading three technical approaches — breakout, trend, and top/bottom trading — each confirmed with the same four-indicator stack. It closes with a “Micro Trading” bonus scalping method for 1-minute charts and basic risk-management guidance.

Key points

  • Standard chart stack: EMA(10, close), EMA(50, close), Bollinger Bands(20, 2, close, exponential middle band), MACD Histogram (12, 26, 9), RSI(14), and Slow Stochastic (5, 3, 3).
  • Trade the Breakout: enter when price breaks out of a Bollinger Band squeeze (bands tightening together), confirmed by price being on the same side of the EMA10/EMA50/BB-middle and by MACD, RSI, and Stochastic all pointing the same direction.
  • Trade the Trend: on a 15-minute chart, if price and the EMA10/BB20 sit below the EMA50, look only for shorts (reverse for longs); enter when price bounces off the EMA10, BB middle, or EMA50 with all momentum indicators confirming.
  • Trading Tops and Bottoms: a counter-trend approach for ranging markets — enter when RSI/MACD/Stochastic turn up from oversold (or down from overbought); flagged as the riskiest of the three approaches.
  • “Micro Trading” bonus system for 1-minute EUR/USD scalps: EMA(3, close) crossing an 18-period Bollinger middle band, confirmed by MACD Histogram and RSI both crossing their zero/50 lines the same direction.
  • Recommended stop-loss placement: 15-20 pips on the visual trading platform used in the course; move the stop to breakeven once a trade is in profit.
  • Position sizing convention: 1 standard lot = 100,000 units, worth roughly $10/pip on EUR/USD and GBP/USD, $8/pip on USD/JPY, and $6/pip on USD/CHF at the time of writing.
  • Hedging feature: opening an equal, opposite position on the same pair to freeze P&L without using extra margin, used as an alternative to a hard stop-loss while deciding whether to exit.
  • News-driven scalping: enter a few minutes after a scheduled economic release, using the 1-minute chart setup to catch the initial price spike.

Actionable rules

  1. Risk per trade: don’t let a stop-loss cost more than 5-7% of total account equity (e.g., $100-$140 max loss on a $2,000 account).
  2. Stop-loss: place 15-20 pips from entry on the intraday setups; tighten to breakeven as soon as the trade is profitable.
  3. Profit target: 5-20 pips per trade on the core system; 30-40 pips/day is cited as a realistic aggregate daily goal (up to ~100+ pips/day for larger risk tolerance).
  4. Entry trigger (breakout): price closes outside a squeezed Bollinger Band with EMA10/EMA50/BB-middle alignment plus MACD, RSI, and Stochastic all confirming direction.
  5. Entry trigger (trend): price bounces off EMA10, BB middle, or EMA50 in the direction the EMA50 already indicates, with momentum indicators confirming.
  6. Micro Trading entry: EMA(3) crosses the 18-period Bollinger middle band with MACD Histogram and RSI crossing their zero/50 lines in the same direction.
  7. Preferred trading windows: the first 3-5 hours of the New York (8am EST), Tokyo/Sydney (7pm EST), or London (3am EST) sessions.

Caveats

This is a self-published marketing course tied to a specific broker referral program (CMS Forex) and a specific charting platform’s terminology (e.g., “Visual Trading”), with broker sign-up instructions embedded in the text; broker recommendations and account-opening mechanics are outdated and not actionable today. No backtest results, win rate, or drawdown statistics are given for any of the three core setups or the bonus scalping method — all guidance is illustrated with a handful of annotated chart examples rather than statistical evidence. Leverage figures cited (200:1, 400:1) reflect mid-2000s retail forex norms well above what most regulated brokers now offer.

Who it is for

Complete beginners to forex day trading who want a concrete, ready-to-use indicator stack and a small set of named setups (breakout, trend, top/bottom, plus a 1-minute scalp) to start practicing on a demo account.

15-Minute Trading Framework

Claudia · Tier B — concise note manual intermediate

A GBP/JPY 15-minute setup combines RSI(5), candlestick reversals, LWMA(5/20), and Parabolic SAR.

Summary

This two-page setup uses a 15-minute candlestick chart with Parabolic SAR, 5- and 20-period linear weighted moving averages, and RSI(5) with levels 10, 20, 70, and 80 (p. 1). Entries require both an extreme RSI reading—preferably above 80 for a short or below 10 for a long—and a visible candlestick reversal pattern. Targets are the first relevant 5 or 20 LWMA, followed by Parabolic SAR after the moving averages cross (p. 1). The author says RSI(5) can warn before other indicators and estimates a 100–125 pip range on 15-minute charts, versus 35–40 pips on five-minute charts, but supplies no measurement method (p. 1). The strategy is used on GBP/JPY, with warnings to avoid major news and avoid countertrend trades unless the candle configuration is compelling (pp. 1–2).

Key points

  • The two-condition entry is intended to reduce single-indicator signals.
  • Timeframe and pair-specific ranges are author claims, not validated targets.

Actionable rules

  1. On GBP/JPY, use 15-minute candles, default Parabolic SAR, close-based LWMA(5) and LWMA(20), and RSI(5) with 10, 20, 70, and 80 levels (p. 1).
  2. Enter only when both conditions occur: RSI is in the stated extreme zone—preferably above 80 for a short or below 10 for a long—and a reversal candle appears (p. 1).
  3. Use the first relevant LWMA as an initial target and, only if the averages cross, the Parabolic SAR or 20 LWMA as the next reference (p. 1).
  4. Avoid news and countertrend entries unless the source’s discretionary candle evidence is present; no stop or sizing rule is supplied (pp. 1–2).

Caveats

No sample, stops, sizing, spread, or performance record is provided. RSI extremes can persist in trends, and fixed pip expectations are not risk controls.

Who it is for

Intraday traders testing a multi-indicator reversal hypothesis.

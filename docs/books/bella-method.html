Bella System

A 30-minute EUR/USD setup waits for RSI or stochastic divergence and a 5/13 EMA cross.

Summary

The Bella system uses a 30-minute EUR/USD chart with RSI(14), stochastic (14,3,3), and 5- and 13-period EMAs (p. 1). The trader first looks for divergence between price and either oscillator, then waits for an EMA cross before entering long or short. The author prefers two lots, taking half after 20–30 pips and moving the remaining stop to breakeven so a trend can extend while some profit is retained (p. 1). A fixed 20–25 pip stop is recommended; the author claims 10–12 opportunities per month and 90% accuracy, without a trade log or sample (p. 1). Major news days should be avoided. The author says GBP/USD may work with a wider 30–40 pip stop and that 15-minute signals produced bad entries while hourly signals arrived late (p. 1).

Key points

  • Divergence is a setup condition, while the EMA cross is the timing condition.
  • Scaling and breakeven management change the payoff distribution and must be tested together.

Actionable rules

  1. On a 30-minute EUR/USD chart, use RSI(14), stochastic (14,3,3), EMA(5), and EMA(13) (p. 1).
  2. Require divergence between price and at least one oscillator, then wait for the EMA cross before entering in its direction (p. 1).
  3. The source prefers two lots, taking one off at +20–30 pips, moving the other stop to breakeven, and starting with a 20–25 pip stop (p. 1).
  4. Avoid major-news days. For GBP/USD the author suggests a 30–40 pip stop, but provides no sizing method or evidence for either fixed distance (p. 1).

Caveats

The 90% accuracy and monthly-opportunity claims are unsupported testimonials. No expectancy, drawdown, or execution evidence is provided.

Who it is for

Traders turning a short system description into a testable specification.

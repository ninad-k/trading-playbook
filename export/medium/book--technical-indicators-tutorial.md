## Summary

An educational article (hosted on ActionForex, credited to TradingEducation.com) surveying the standard toolkit of technical indicators in four families: strength/sentiment (volume, open interest, COT reports), trend (moving averages, MACD, DMI/ADX), volatility (Bollinger Bands), and momentum/oscillators (Stochastics, RSI, CCI, Williams %R, rate-of-change). For each it gives the definition, calculation logic in words, and the conventional signal rules. It opens with a warning that no single oscillator is a complete system and that oscillators specifically perform poorly in strongly trending markets.

## Key points

- **Volume and open interest** confirm moves: rising price with rising open interest is bullish (new money entering); rising price with falling open interest suggests short covering and a fizzling rally.
- **COT report**: commercials are treated as "smart money"; net commercial position changes are the most-watched line in the weekly CFTC report.
- **Moving averages**: simple, weighted, or exponential; a shorter average crossing a longer one is the standard buy/sell trigger.
- **MACD**: difference of a longer and shorter EMA (example: 21/9) with a signal-line crossover, zero-line crossover, and divergence as three distinct signal types; lags and needs sustained moves.
- **DMI/ADX**: long on +DI crossing above −DI, short on the reverse; ADX above 30 signals a strong (direction-agnostic) trend, below 30 signals chop.
- **Bollinger Bands**: bands set at a number of standard deviations from a moving average; width reflects volatility; often paired with RSI so a band touch with no RSI confirmation flags a reversal.
- **Stochastics (%K/%D)**: overbought above 80, oversold below 20; highest-confidence signal is a right-side %K/%D crossover combined with %D divergence against price.
- **RSI**: 14-period standard, overbought above 70, oversold below 30; a failure-swing divergence against price is stronger than the raw level alone.
- **CCI**: buy above +100, exit below it; sell below −100, cover near −85; about 70-80% of price action stays within ±100.
- **Williams %R**: inverted 0-100 scale; above 80 is oversold, below 20 overbought.

## Actionable rules

1. Confirm breakouts with open-interest direction: rising open interest on the breakout favors continuation.
2. Trade moving-average crossovers (common sets: 5/10/20-day) with the shorter average crossing the longer one.
3. Treat MACD's signal-line cross, zero-line cross, and divergence as three separate signals, not one.
4. Only trade DMI +DI/−DI crossovers when ADX is above roughly 30.
5. Only treat a Bollinger Band touch as a reversal if RSI fails to confirm the new price extreme.
6. Use CCI's ±100 crossings (not overbought/oversold framing) as its buy/sell trigger.

# Sure-Fire Forex Trading: the trader's summary

*Multi-timeframe forex method combining 89/144-period EMA trend filters with 38.2%/61.8% Fibonacci entries and strict 2:1+ risk/reward, 3%-of-equity money management.*

**Mark McRae** · 2005 · Forex Mechanics & Macro Drivers · beginner

*Source coverage: partial. PDF pages inspected: 15-16, 55-56, 59-60 (the quoting mechanics and the Fibonacci target formulas with worked examples). These are study notes, not verified trading results.*

## Summary

A beginner-to-intermediate forex ebook that opens with market mechanics (players, spot/forward/futures/options, leverage, margin, pairs) before presenting a specific discretionary-mechanical method: identify trend with two EMA "bands" across multiple timeframes, time entries with Fibonacci retracements, and lean heavily on probability/money-management math. The book argues money management, not the entry signal, separates surviving traders from blown accounts.

## Key points

- Uses three nested timeframes (e.g., 4-hour, 30-minute, 5-minute): largest sets trend, middle times entries, smallest fine-tunes/monitors.
- Trend defined by two EMA bands: 89-period EMA of highs/lows (short-term) and 144-period EMA of highs/lows (long-term) — both Fibonacci numbers, chosen for fitting FX price action.
- A "Trend Indicator" (TI) built from swing points (two consecutive higher highs = swing up; lower lows = swing down) flips only when the most recent swing point is breached.
- Entries use only two Fibonacci levels, 38.2% and 61.8% (not 50%), measured between swing points A and B.
- Three targets from points A, B, C: Target1 = 0.618×(B−A)+C; Target2 = (B−A)+C; Target3 = 1.618×(B−A)+C.
- Explains why win/loss sequences are skewed and unpredictable (coin-flip analogy), and why doubling up after a loss is catastrophic (10 consecutive doubles from $100 loses $102,300 total).
- Drawdown-recovery table: a 50% loss requires a 100% gain to recover; a 20% loss requires 25%.
- A "Risk Probability Calculator" flags a setup as tradeable only when potential reward is at least 2x the risk.

## Actionable rules

1. Long setup: both EMA bands must be in "buy mode" (89s crossed above 144s) on both the larger and entry timeframe.
2. The Trend Indicator must be in or turning to buy mode (higher highs/higher lows structure).
3. Enter only on a 38.2% Fibonacci retracement; place the stop just beyond the 61.8% retracement level.
4. Take only trades with reward at least 2–3x the risk (2:1 minimum, 3:1 preferred).
5. Risk no more than 3% of equity per trade, and cap total combined risk across simultaneous open trades at 3% as well (many experienced traders cited use 1%).
6. Mirror all rules for shorts (EMA bands crossed down, TI in sell mode, retracement entry from above).

## Caveats

Self-published ebook with a standard risk disclaimer and no independently verified track record or backtest for the 89/144 EMA combination — the choice is justified by fit, not published testing. Heavy reliance on discretionary swing-point identification, subjective in real time by the author's own admission for similar methods. The money-management/probability material is sound, standard risk-of-ruin reasoning.

## Who it is for

Beginner-to-intermediate forex traders who want a complete, self-contained trend-following framework plus a serious grounding in position-sizing and drawdown math.

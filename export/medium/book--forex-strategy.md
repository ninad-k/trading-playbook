# Strategy:10: the trader's summary

*Rob Booker’s preview targets a disciplined 10-pip daily objective with mandatory stops, limits, multi-timeframe review, and a five-hour cap.*

**Rob Booker** · 2003 · Forex Mechanics & Macro Drivers · intermediate

*Source coverage: full. PDF pages inspected: 1-11. These are study notes, not verified trading results.*

## Summary

Booker’s Strategy:10 preview describes a short-horizon forex routine built around modest daily targets, predefined exits, and emotional discipline. It explains pips and leverage, noting that brokers historically offered 50:1 to 100:1 margin and sometimes as much as 400:1 (pp. 4-5). The method aims for 10 pips per day, uses stops and limits, and relies on half-hour, three-hour, daily, weekly, and monthly charts (pp. 7-9). The guide says a trader should stop pursuing a makeup trade after a loss, keep trying for the daily objective only while losses remain under 5% of capital, and limit active screen time to five hours (p. 9). A daily routine checks economic news, overbought/oversold conditions, support, resistance, and worst-case loss (p. 10).

## Key points

- The author frames greed and revenge trading as major threats (p. 6).
- A $10,000 position is shown as $1 per pip, with larger notional amounts scaling linearly (p. 5).
- The example stop is 20 pips below entry and the example limit is 100 pips above, illustrating order mechanics rather than a fixed system ratio (p. 8).
- Breakouts are the stated entry style in the seven principles (p. 9).
- The 10-pip objective and 5% loss boundary are author rules, not validated population statistics.

## Actionable rules

1. Use a protective stop and profit limit on every trade (p. 8).
2. Target 10 pips per day and stop pursuing a larger recovery target after a loss (p. 9).
3. Cap active trading at five hours and review multiple chart horizons before entering (pp. 8-10).

## Caveats

This is a promotional preview and includes high historical leverage examples. The entry criteria are incomplete, and compounding claims are hypothetical. Current margin, pip values, and broker rules differ.

## Who it is for

Forex beginners who need a simple routine and explicit emotional and order-management boundaries.

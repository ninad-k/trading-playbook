# IQ Trader Candlestick Book: the trader's summary

*A purely definitional catalogue of 30 candlestick patterns and 22 chart patterns, one paragraph each, with no entry, stop-loss, target, or trend-context rules anywhere in the book.*

**IQ Trader** · Candlesticks & Chart Patterns · beginner

*Source coverage: full. PDF pages inspected: 1-57 (full read). These are study notes, not verified trading results.*

## Summary

The thinnest of this batch's five shorter titles: a straight glossary of 30 candlestick patterns followed by 22 chart patterns, each given exactly one short descriptive paragraph (typically three to five sentences) and nothing else — no entry rule, no stop-loss, no target, no explicit trend-context requirement, and no worked example anywhere in the book. Branded to the "@iq_trader_07" handle with the same standard educational-purpose disclaimer and social-media links seen across this batch's other IQ Trader titles.

## Key points

- **Candlestick catalogue (30 entries)** — Hammer, Inverted Hammer, Gravestone/Dragonfly Doji, Spinning Top, Morning/Evening Star, Shooting Star, Hanging Man, Bullish/Bearish Engulfing, Tweezer Bottom/Top, Bullish/Bearish Inside Bar, Bullish/Bearish Harami, Three White Soldiers, Three Black Crows, Dark Cloud Cover, Pin Bar, Piercing Bullish, Bullish/Bearish Kicker, Three Outside Up/Down, Three Inside Up/Down, Bullish/Bearish Marubozu.
- **Chart pattern catalogue (22 entries)** — Double/Triple Top/Bottom, Bullish/Bearish Pennant, Bullish/Bearish Flag, Bullish/Bearish Rectangle, Rising/Falling Wedge, Head and Shoulders (and inverted), Symmetrical/Broadening/Ascending/Descending Triangle, Cup and Handle, Rounding Bottom, Diamond Top/Bottom.
- **Format**: every entry is definitional only — what the shape looks like and what it is generally believed to signal (e.g., "a bullish harami is... used for spotting reversals in a bear trend") — with no formation-timing qualifier, no entry trigger, and no stop-loss or target logic given for any of the 52 patterns.

## Actionable rules

The book states none. Unlike this batch's other IQ Trader titles (which at minimum pair most patterns with an entry-above/below-the-signal-candle and a stop-at-the-pattern's-extreme instruction), this text never specifies an entry price, a stop-loss placement, a target, or even a required prior trend for most patterns — a handful of entries mention trend context in passing (e.g., "a hanging man occurs during an uptrend") but even these stop short of a tradeable rule. A reader wanting to act on any pattern in this book must supply the standard entry/stop/target logic themselves from another source; none of it should be assumed to be implied.

## Caveats

This is a definitions-only reference with no actionable content, no worked examples, no numeric parameters, and no stated win rates or backtests — it should not be mistaken for a trading method. It duplicates, almost pattern-for-pattern, the catalogues in this batch's other IQ Trader titles and in several other candlestick references already in this library, adding no new pattern coverage. As with sibling titles, embedded social-media handles and a boilerplate educational disclaimer mark it as a self-published lead-generation product rather than an edited or reviewed trading text.

## Who it is for

Only useful as a quick-glance visual/verbal glossary for someone who has forgotten what a specific pattern's shape or general bias is; a reader who wants any usable entry, stop, or target logic should use a different reference in this library instead, including this batch's own `iq-trader-ultimate-trading-playbook` or `charts-dont-lie`, both of which pair the same style of catalogue with actual trade rules.

## Summary

This is a single-page forum post in which a trader nicknamed "Maitei" shares a simple two-moving-average forex system. It uses a 55-period EMA to define the prevailing trend and a 16-period EMA to time entries, confirmed by candle closes rather than the moving average line alone. The author presents it as a beginner-friendly system with no supporting statistics, backtests, or examples.

## Key points

- Two exponential moving averages are used: a 55 EMA (slower, defines trend) and a 16 EMA (faster, generates the signal).
- Price above the 55 EMA defines an uptrend; price below it defines a downtrend.
- The trader should only look for buy signals in an uptrend and only sell signals in a downtrend.
- A signal requires waiting for a candle to close beyond the 16 EMA in the direction of the prevailing trend, rather than acting on an intrabar touch.
- The author states the system can be applied on multiple timeframes, with the appropriate timeframe depending on the currency pair traded.
- No stop-loss, target, position sizing, or risk management guidance is given anywhere in the post.

## Actionable rules

1. Determine trend direction using the 55 EMA: price above it means only take longs; price below it means only take shorts.
2. For a long: wait for a candlestick to close above the 16 EMA while price is above the 55 EMA.
3. For a short: wait for a candlestick to close below the 16 EMA while price is below the 55 EMA.
4. Do not take counter-trend signals (e.g. no buying while price is below the 55 EMA).
5. Stop-loss, profit target, and position sizing: none given.

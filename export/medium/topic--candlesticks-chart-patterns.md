# Candlesticks & Chart Patterns: a trading-library study guide

*Pattern recognition from candle anatomy to flags and head-and-shoulders: the library agrees patterns need trend context and confirmation, and rarely tests whether they work.*

## What it is

Candlestick and chart-pattern analysis reads recurring shapes in price as evidence about what buyers and sellers just did. Candlesticks work at the scale of one to a few sessions, encoding the relationship between open, high, low and close — a long body shows conviction, a long shadow shows a price rejected ([Introduction to Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trading-hill-arthur-introduction-to-candlesticks.html), [Beyond Candlesticks: New Japanese Charting Techniques Revealed](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/beyond-candlesticks-steve-nison.html)). Western chart patterns work at the scale of weeks: flags, triangles, double tops and head-and-shoulders formations, most of which carry a measured-move target derived from the pattern's own dimensions ([Chart Patterns and Technical Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-and-technical-indicators.html)). The two traditions are usually combined, because candles pinpoint a session but supply no target, while chart patterns supply a target but not a precise entry bar. Almost every source in this category insists a pattern means nothing without the trend that precedes it: the same shape is a hammer after a decline and a hanging man after an advance.

## Core principles

- Trend context is part of the pattern definition, not an optional filter — identical shapes carry opposite meanings depending on what preceded them [Introduction to Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trading-hill-arthur-introduction-to-candlesticks.html) [An Introduction to Japanese Candlestick Charting](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/understanding-forex-candlestick.html) [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html).
- Most single- and two-candle signals require confirmation within the next one to three sessions before they are acted on; three-candle star patterns are treated as strong enough to stand alone [Introduction to Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trading-hill-arthur-introduction-to-candlesticks.html) [17 Money Making Candlestick Formations](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/17-money-making-candlestick-formations2.html).
- Candles identify reversal sessions but supply neither a price target nor a stop, so they need a second tool for exits [Beyond Candlesticks: New Japanese Charting Techniques Revealed](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/beyond-candlesticks-steve-nison.html) [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html).
- Signals gain reliability at confluence — a pattern landing on an independently derived level (pivot, moving average, Fibonacci retracement, prior swing) rather than in open space [A Complete Guide to Technical Trading Tactics: How to Profit Using Pivot Points, Candlesticks & Other Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-complete-guide-to-technical-trading-tactics-2004.html) [A Realistic and Effective Strategy for Using Candlestick Charts](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-realistic-and-effective-strategy-for-using-candlestick-sri-0032.html) [Forex Trading with Candlesticks and Patterns, Part 2](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/forex-trading-with-candlestick-and-pattern-2.html).
- Filtering candle signals through an oscillator's overbought/oversold zone improves results over either tool alone, mainly by cutting trade count and raising average gain per trade [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html) [Stock Investing Basics of Japanese Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/jp-candlesticks.html).
- Subjective pattern recognition can be replaced with numeric thresholds — body size relative to recent range, shadow length as a multiple of the body, penetration percentage — which is what makes patterns testable at all [Candlestick Charting Explained — Pattern Catalogue and Reliability Ranking](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained--pattern-catalogue-and-reliability.html) [Detecting Breakouts From Flags & Pennants](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/detecting-breakouts-from-flags-pennants.html).
- When patterns are actually tested, individual reliability is modest: roughly half of the catalogue beats its own baseline, and continuation patterns face a higher bar because trends persist anyway [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html).
- Chart patterns carry measured-move targets computed from their own geometry — flagpole height, triangle width, head-to-neckline distance — projected from the breakout [Chart Patterns and Technical Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-and-technical-indicators.html) [Chart Patterns Tutorial](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-tutorial.html) [Double Tops & Double Bottoms](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/doubletopsandbottoms.html).
- A pattern is not complete until price closes beyond its boundary; an intraday touch of a neckline or trendline is not a signal [Double Tops & Double Bottoms](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/doubletopsandbottoms.html) [Chart Patterns Tutorial](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-tutorial.html).
- Volume behaviour is part of several Western patterns' definitions: contraction while the pattern forms, expansion on the break [Chart Patterns and Technical Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-and-technical-indicators.html) [Detecting Breakouts From Flags & Pennants](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/detecting-breakouts-from-flags-pennants.html) [7 Chart Patterns That Consistently Make Money](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trade-7-chart-patterns-that-consistantly-make-money-downs.html).
- Patterns fail routinely, and the trader should be prepared to trade the failure rather than assume the expected resolution [7 Chart Patterns That Consistently Make Money](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trade-7-chart-patterns-that-consistantly-make-money-downs.html) [Chart Formations](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-formations.html) [Forex Trading with Candlesticks and Patterns, Part 1](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/candlestick-and-pattern-1.html).
- Broken support tends to act as resistance afterwards and vice versa, which is what gives retested levels their significance [Chart Patterns Tutorial](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-tutorial.html) [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/candlestick-charting-explained.html).
- Alternative Japanese charts — three-line break, renko, kagi — print only on new highs or lows, which strips out sideways noise and makes trend and support/resistance visually explicit; the recommended workflow is to set trend on one of these and trigger on candles [Beyond Candlesticks: New Japanese Charting Techniques Revealed](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/beyond-candlesticks-steve-nison.html).
- Intraday forex rarely gaps, so gap-dependent pattern definitions must be relaxed or dropped in that market [Forex Trading with Candlesticks and Patterns, Part 1](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/candlestick-and-pattern-1.html) [Chart Formations](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-formations.html).

## Concrete rules and setups

### Candlestick recognition thresholds

1. Hammer, hanging man, shooting star and inverted hammer: lower (or upper) shadow at least twice the real body, with little or no opposite shadow — no more than roughly 5-10% of the day's range [Candlestick Charting Explained — Pattern Catalogue and Reliability Ranking](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained--pattern-catalogue-and-reliability.html) [Introduction to Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trading-hill-arthur-introduction-to-candlesticks.html). A shadow of three times the body or more reclassifies it as the stronger Takuri line [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html).
2. Doji: absolute open-to-close distance under roughly 1-3% of the day's high-low range, and only meaningful after a run of non-doji sessions in an established trend [Candlestick Charting Explained — Pattern Catalogue and Reliability Ranking](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained--pattern-catalogue-and-reliability.html).
3. Engulfing: a trend must be in force, the second body must fully cover the first, and the second candle's colour must oppose the trend; penetration of at least 30% beyond the first body strengthens it, and shadows are ignored [Candlestick Charting Explained — Pattern Catalogue and Reliability Ranking](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained--pattern-catalogue-and-reliability.html) [Forex Trading with Candlesticks and Patterns, Part 1](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/candlestick-and-pattern-1.html).
4. Piercing line and dark cloud cover: day two opens beyond day one's extreme and closes past the midpoint of day one's body. Morris allows no flexibility on the 50% rule — less penetration reclassifies the pair as a weaker continuation pattern (on-neck, in-neck, thrusting line) [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html) [17 Money Making Candlestick Formations](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/17-money-making-candlestick-formations2.html).
5. Trend context for pattern qualification: Morris uses a 10-day EMA of closes, Hill accepts price below a 20-day EMA, successively lower swing peaks and troughs, or a broken trendline [Candlestick Charting Explained — Pattern Catalogue and Reliability Ranking](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained--pattern-catalogue-and-reliability.html) [Introduction to Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trading-hill-arthur-introduction-to-candlesticks.html).
6. Blending: reduce a multi-candle pattern to one synthetic candle using the first open, the last close and the pattern's high and low — a bullish engulfing or piercing pattern blends into a hammer [Introduction to Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trading-hill-arthur-introduction-to-candlesticks.html).

### Alternative Japanese charts

7. Disparity index = ((close − MA) ÷ MA) × 100. Roughly +10% to +15% is commonly overbought and −10% to −15% oversold, market-dependent; a price high against a lower disparity peak is bearish divergence [The Disparity Index](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/beyond-candlesticks-steve-nison--disparity-index.html).
8. Three-line break: draw a line only on a new closing high or low. Once three lines share a colour, a reversal requires a close beyond the extreme of all three, not merely a new high or low [Three-Line Break Charts](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/beyond-candlesticks-steve-nison--three-line-break-chart.html).
9. Renko: fixed brick height; a brick prints only when price closes a full brick beyond the current extreme. Buy on a white brick, sell on a black one — a pure stop-and-reverse system [Renko Charts](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/beyond-candlesticks-steve-nison--renko-chart.html).
10. Kagi: fixed turnaround amount, around 3% for Japanese stocks as a starting convention. Buy when the line thickens through a prior shoulder, sell when it thins through a prior waist; two- or three-level break variants trade less often at higher confidence [Kagi Charts](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/beyond-candlesticks-steve-nison--kagi-chart.html).

### Western chart patterns

11. Flags and pennants: require a sharp high-volume flagpole; the pattern should resolve within about 20 sessions; target = flagpole height added to the breakout [Chart Patterns and Technical Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-and-technical-indicators.html) [Chart Patterns Tutorial](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-tutorial.html).
12. Katsanos's coded flag/pennant filter: flag duration 2-21 days, flagpole slope at least 2.2%/day on a 13-bar regression, flag slope between −1.2% and +0.2%/day, declining volume and volatility, 20-day stochastic above 55, 10-day ADX above 30, and a close above both the prior close and the day's open [Detecting Breakouts From Flags & Pennants](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/detecting-breakouts-from-flags-pennants.html).
13. Katsanos's exits: profit target = 1.94 × (pole height %)^0.724; stop on a close below the flag's lower trendline; exit for inactivity after 14 days if profit is under 25% of target; trail 10% below the highest close of the last four days; force exit at 24 days [Detecting Breakouts From Flags & Pennants](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/detecting-breakouts-from-flags-pennants.html).
14. Triangles: enter on a close beyond the boundary occurring between roughly two-thirds and three-quarters of the way to the apex, with volume contracting during formation and expanding on the break; target = the triangle's widest height from the breakout [Chart Patterns and Technical Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-and-technical-indicators.html). Person notes that the longer a triangle takes to form, the weaker the eventual breakout tends to be, with typical daily-chart triangles running 6-10 sessions [A Complete Guide to Technical Trading Tactics: How to Profit Using Pivot Points, Candlesticks & Other Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-complete-guide-to-technical-trading-tactics-2004.html).
15. Double tops and bottoms: confirmed only on a close through the neckline. Target = neckline − (C − B) for a top, neckline + (B − C) for a bottom; stop between the neckline and the intervening peak or trough [Double Tops & Double Bottoms](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/doubletopsandbottoms.html).
16. Head-and-shoulders: the neckline break confirms; project the head-to-neckline distance from the break [A Complete Guide to Technical Trading Tactics: How to Profit Using Pivot Points, Candlesticks & Other Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-complete-guide-to-technical-trading-tactics-2004.html) [Chart Patterns and Technical Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-and-technical-indicators.html).
17. Gap taxonomy: breakaway gaps start a move, measuring or continuation gaps mark its middle, exhaustion gaps mark its end; a breakaway, midpoint and exhaustion gap in sequence, or an island reversal, marks a likely termination [A Complete Guide to Technical Trading Tactics: How to Profit Using Pivot Points, Candlesticks & Other Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-complete-guide-to-technical-trading-tactics-2004.html) [Chart Patterns Tutorial](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-tutorial.html) [7 Chart Patterns That Consistently Make Money](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trade-7-chart-patterns-that-consistantly-make-money-downs.html).

### Confluence entries

18. P3T: calculate P = (H + L + C) ÷ 3, R1 = 2P − L, R2 = (P + H) − L, S1 = 2P − H, S2 = (P − H) + L on daily, weekly and monthly data in parallel. Trade only the first test of a level, require a candlestick reversal there, and add an oscillator divergence or lopsided Commitments of Traders positioning where available [P3T: Pivot Point + Candlestick Confluence Trading](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-complete-guide-to-technical-trading-tactics-2004--p3t-pivot-candle-confluence.html).
19. The CI System: read weekly through hourly candles, mark pivots, trendlines and Fibonacci levels, then enter on a 15-minute engulfing candle only when the stochastic has crossed in the trade direction, using a 5/13 EMA cross on the 5-minute chart as secondary confirmation. Exit on an opposite engulfing candle, a rejection at a marked level, a Fibonacci target or an adverse EMA cross [Forex Trading with Candlesticks and Patterns, Part 2](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/forex-trading-with-candlestick-and-pattern-2.html).
20. Pullback entry sequence: identify the trend, wait for a retracement to a Fibonacci level, pivot, trendline or prior extreme, then buy one pip above the high of the pullback's pivot-low bar once the following bar confirms it, with the stop beyond that pivot [Forex Trading with Candlesticks and Patterns, Part 1](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/candlestick-and-pattern-1.html).
21. Bigalow's bottom setup: an oversold stochastic plus a bullish reversal candle plus a gap up is treated as confirmation of demand at a low; the J-Hook adds a pullback that does not reach oversold before price hooks back through the prior high [Big Profit Patterns Using Candlestick Signals and Gaps](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/big-profit-patterns-using-candlestick-signals-and-gaps-stephen-w-bigalow.html) [Stock Investing Basics of Japanese Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/jp-candlesticks.html).
22. Downs's rule set across all seven of his patterns: establish a reference scale first, trail stops using the eighths (38%, 50%, 62%), and reject any setup offering less than 2:1 reward to risk, preferably 3:1 [7 Chart Patterns That Consistently Make Money](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trade-7-chart-patterns-that-consistantly-make-money-downs.html).
23. Stops sit on the far side of the pattern's extreme — below a hammer's low, above a hanging man's high, beyond a chart pattern's boundary — rather than at an arbitrary dollar figure [Beyond Candlesticks: New Japanese Charting Techniques Revealed](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/beyond-candlesticks-steve-nison.html) [A Complete Guide to Technical Trading Tactics: How to Profit Using Pivot Points, Candlesticks & Other Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-complete-guide-to-technical-trading-tactics-2004.html).

## Common mistakes

- Trading a shape without the prior trend it requires, which is the single most common error the primers warn about [An Introduction to Japanese Candlestick Charting](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/understanding-forex-candlestick.html) [Introduction to Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trading-hill-arthur-introduction-to-candlesticks.html) [A Realistic and Effective Strategy for Using Candlestick Charts](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-realistic-and-effective-strategy-for-using-candlestick-sri-0032.html).
- Acting on a single pattern in isolation instead of requiring independent agreement — Person's "verify, verify, verify" [A Complete Guide to Technical Trading Tactics: How to Profit Using Pivot Points, Candlesticks & Other Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-complete-guide-to-technical-trading-tactics-2004.html) [A Realistic and Effective Strategy for Using Candlestick Charts](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-realistic-and-effective-strategy-for-using-candlestick-sri-0032.html).
- Skipping the required confirmation session on hammers, hanging men and inverted hammers [17 Money Making Candlestick Formations](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/17-money-making-candlestick-formations2.html) [Introduction to Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trading-hill-arthur-introduction-to-candlesticks.html).
- Treating an intraday penetration of a neckline or trendline as a completed pattern rather than waiting for the close [Double Tops & Double Bottoms](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/doubletopsandbottoms.html) [Chart Patterns Tutorial](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-tutorial.html).
- Using vague thresholds so that two traders identify different patterns in the same data; Morris shows classification is highly sensitive to where the long/short/doji cutoffs are set [Candlestick Charting Explained — Pattern Catalogue and Reliability Ranking](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained--pattern-catalogue-and-reliability.html).
- Trusting reported success rates that come from selected charts rather than a sample — including the explicitly "unofficial" 80% figure one source attaches to its own setup [Big Profit Patterns Using Candlestick Signals and Gaps](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/big-profit-patterns-using-candlestick-signals-and-gaps-stephen-w-bigalow.html) [Stock Investing Basics of Japanese Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/jp-candlesticks.html).
- Reading a high raw success rate as an edge when the relevant comparison is the base rate of the trend continuing or reversing anyway [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html).
- Ignoring costs: Morris notes an average 0.6% gain per trade disappears once commissions and slippage are counted [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html).
- Re-entering on the second or third test of the same pivot level, after the edge has eroded [P3T: Pivot Point + Candlestick Confluence Trading](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-complete-guide-to-technical-trading-tactics-2004--p3t-pivot-candle-confluence.html).
- Switching timeframes mid-trade after choosing one to fit the pattern's visible size [7 Chart Patterns That Consistently Make Money](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trade-7-chart-patterns-that-consistantly-make-money-downs.html).

## Best books for this topic

1. [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html) — the only source here that assigns numeric recognition rules and then tests over 60 patterns against a baseline. Essential for anyone who wants to know whether candles work rather than what they are called.
2. [Beyond Candlesticks: New Japanese Charting Techniques Revealed](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/beyond-candlesticks-steve-nison.html) — the three-line break, renko and kagi construction chapters are built from worked numeric tables and are unambiguous enough to code. For traders who need trend context and exits that candles cannot supply.
3. [A Complete Guide to Technical Trading Tactics: How to Profit Using Pivot Points, Candlesticks & Other Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-complete-guide-to-technical-trading-tactics-2004.html) — pivot formulas stated exactly and applied across timeframes, with candlesticks as the confirming layer. The most complete confluence framework in the category.
4. [Introduction to Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trading-hill-arthur-introduction-to-candlesticks.html) — the clearest structured primer: construction, the full reversal set, and explicit confirmation and trend-definition rules. The best starting point.
5. [Detecting Breakouts From Flags & Pennants](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/detecting-breakouts-from-flags-pennants.html) — a fully coded flag and pennant system with a derived target formula; a model of how to make a chart pattern mechanical.
6. [Chart Patterns and Technical Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-and-technical-indicators.html) — the most complete single reference for Western patterns with durations, volume requirements and target formulas.
7. [Forex Trading with Candlesticks and Patterns, Part 1](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/candlestick-and-pattern-1.html) and [Forex Trading with Candlesticks and Patterns, Part 2](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/forex-trading-with-candlestick-and-pattern-2.html) — a two-part course carrying the material through to a trade plan, useful where gap-based definitions break down.
8. [Double Tops & Double Bottoms](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/doubletopsandbottoms.html) — three pages, but the neckline-confirmation and measured-move rules are stated more precisely than in most longer treatments.
9. [17 Money Making Candlestick Formations](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/17-money-making-candlestick-formations2.html) — a compact illustrated glossary for looking up a shape quickly. Reference only; there is no system here.

## Open debates

- **Whether candlestick patterns have a measurable edge.** Morris's own testing finds roughly half the catalogue beats its baseline, with rankings shifting materially across 3-, 5- and 7-day horizons, and several perfect-scoring patterns occurring only once or twice [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html). Most other sources in this category assert reliability through selected charts and give no sample at all [Stock Investing Basics of Japanese Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/jp-candlesticks.html) [Chart Formations](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-formations.html) [Candlestick Patterns for Day Trading](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/candlestick-patterns-for-day-trading.html).
- **Why they would work.** Bigalow argues candles are self-fulfilling — enough traders watch them that the crowd response validates the signal [Stock Investing Basics of Japanese Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/jp-candlesticks.html). Morris treats them instead as an encoding of short-term trader psychology at turning points, testable like any other signal [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html). Downs points to academic work finding patterns occur more often than chance would predict, without claiming they are tradable after costs [7 Chart Patterns That Consistently Make Money](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trade-7-chart-patterns-that-consistantly-make-money-downs.html).
- **Whether candlestick names add anything over bar charts.** Kamada argues the named patterns are not inherently better than the equivalent bar shapes and that the value lies entirely in the surrounding context [A Realistic and Effective Strategy for Using Candlestick Charts](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-realistic-and-effective-strategy-for-using-candlestick-sri-0032.html); Nison's case is that the open-to-close relationship is genuinely additional information [Beyond Candlesticks: New Japanese Charting Techniques Revealed](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/beyond-candlesticks-steve-nison.html).
- **How strict the penetration rules should be.** Morris treats the 50% close on piercing and dark cloud cover as non-negotiable, reclassifying anything less [Candlestick Charting Explained](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/greg-morris-candlestick-charting-explained.html); other sources accept "well into the body" without quantifying it, which makes their signals unreproducible [Stock Investing Basics of Japanese Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/jp-candlesticks.html) [17 Money Making Candlestick Formations](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/17-money-making-candlestick-formations2.html).
- **Confirmation versus timeliness.** Requiring a confirming session avoids acting on shapes that resolve into nothing [Introduction to Candlesticks](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trading-hill-arthur-introduction-to-candlesticks.html), but Nison's new-price charts only confirm on the close, by which point price may be far past the reversal threshold — some Japanese traders take a light position at the touch and resolve it at the close [Beyond Candlesticks: New Japanese Charting Techniques Revealed](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/beyond-candlesticks-steve-nison.html).
- **Fade the level or trade the break.** Person's method treats a pivot as a place to fade when a reversal candle appears there, with the candle deciding direction [P3T: Pivot Point + Candlestick Confluence Trading](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/a-complete-guide-to-technical-trading-tactics-2004--p3t-pivot-candle-confluence.html); the breakout literature treats the same boundaries as places to enter in the direction of the break [Chart Formations](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-formations.html) [Chart Patterns and Technical Indicators](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/chart-patterns-and-technical-indicators.html).
- **How much risk a confluence setup justifies.** The CI System caps single-trade risk at 7% of equity, far above the 1-2% typical elsewhere in this library, on the strength of multiple confirmations [Forex Trading with Candlesticks and Patterns, Part 2](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/forex-trading-with-candlestick-and-pattern-2.html); Downs instead constrains by reward-to-risk ratio and says nothing about equity percentage [7 Chart Patterns That Consistently Make Money](https://ninad-k.github.io/trading-playbook/books/trade-7-chart-patterns-that-consistantly-make-money-downs.html).

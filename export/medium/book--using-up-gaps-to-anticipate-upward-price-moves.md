# Using Upward Gaps to Predict Upward Trends: the trader's summary

*A gap strategy scans for low-volatility stocks whose upward gaps have abnormal volume and sufficient dollar liquidity.*

**Stockscores.com** · 1999 · Market Structure & Price Action · intermediate

*Source coverage: full. PDF pages inspected: 1-3. These are study notes, not verified trading results.*

## Summary

Stockscores defines an upward gap as today’s low being above yesterday’s high (p. 1). It interprets the gap as evidence of strong demand that may begin or reverse a trend, but warns that thin liquidity can create a gap without meaningful information. The scan therefore combines the gap with abnormal volume, low prior volatility, and a minimum dollar-volume filter of $250,000 (p. 2). The visual examples focus on a gap breaking a range or a downward trendline. The bottom of the gap is described as a possible support area; penetration can invalidate the signal and justify accepting a loss (p. 3).

## Key points

- Gap and volume should be interpreted together (pp. 1-2).
- Low-volatility consolidation can precede a new move (p. 2).
- A gap through resistance and a gap breaking a downtrend are separate contexts (p. 2).
- Thinly traded gaps are specifically discouraged (pp. 1, 3).
- The source states no strategy works 100% of the time (p. 3).

## Actionable rules

1. Require today’s low > yesterday’s high (p. 1).
2. Add abnormal volume, yesterday’s low-volatility condition, and dollar volume >= $250,000 to the scan (p. 2).
3. Monitor the gap bottom as support and reassess if price penetrates it (p. 3).

## Caveats

This 1999 scan is a historical stock-screening method. “Abnormal volume” and low volatility depend on the platform’s definitions, and no expectancy table is supplied.

## Who it is for

Swing traders learning to combine gap structure with liquidity and volume filters.

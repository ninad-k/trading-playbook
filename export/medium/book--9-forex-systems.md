# 9 Forex Systems: the trader's summary

*Compilation of nine short intraday forex systems (EMA/Parabolic SAR crosses, scalping, MACD/RSI/Stochastic combos, pivot points, news-jump trading) pulled from various online forex educators.*

**Various** · 2006 · Forex Mechanics & Macro Drivers · beginner

*Source coverage: partial. PDF pages inspected: 2, 4, 8-9 (the money-management section and the individual system rules). These are study notes, not verified trading results.*

## Summary

A compiled manual of nine separate short-term/intraday forex approaches, each a few pages, covering moving-average crossovers, 1-minute scalping, a candlestick/stochastic "CI System," Tom DeMark trend lines, economic-news-jump trading, and intraday pivot trading. It opens with general "foundations" (follow 1-2 major pairs, always use stops, don't over-leverage, trade with a written plan) that apply across all nine.

## Key points

- **Forex Profit System** — 60-min chart, EMA 10/25/50 + Parabolic SAR (.02/.2); enter on EMA 10 crossing 25 and 50 with SAR confirming; exit on price crossing back through all three EMAs; stop below/above EMA 50.
- **1-minute scalp variant** — same crossover logic with EMA 25/50/100, targeting 5-10 pips, best at London (3am EST) or New York (8am EST) opens.
- **Moving Average Intraday System** — 30-min chart, 11-day MA; signal triggers only when the MA crosses through the body (not the wick) of a candle, confirmed by MACD zero-line position.
- **The CI System** — 15/30/60-min engulfing/star candlestick entries confirmed by a 15,3,3 Stochastic cross, plus trendlines and Fibonacci levels for targets; stop 10-15 pips beyond the pivot; target ≥1.5x the stop.
- **Tom DeMark FX System** — hourly chart, EMA 9/30 cross plus a momentum indicator crossing 100, filtered by a TD trend-line break; 40-pip stop, 40-150 pip target, trailed in 10-pip steps.
- **Forex News Trading** — trades the first minutes around major releases (NFP, CPI, GDP, PPI, retail sales) with a tight (~10-15 pip) stop and fixed pip target.
- **Intraday Pivots System** — daily pivot/R1-2/S1-2/M1-4 levels (3pm EST cycle), traded by price action breaking/retesting a level, with 9/18 EMA and MACD zero-cross as secondary confirmation.

## Actionable rules

1. Trade during session overlaps (Asian/European 2-4am EST; European/US 8am-12pm EST) — flagged as highest-probability windows.
2. Close all positions by early Friday afternoon to avoid weekend gap risk.
3. Use no more than ~10% of account equity as margin at any time.
4. Target ≥1.5x the stop distance on the CI System and pivot-based trades.
5. Tom DeMark FX System: 40-pip stop, 40-150 pip target, 10-pip trailing steps.

## Caveats

A self-published compilation of retail forex "systems" from around 2006 with no aggregated backtests, no sample sizes, and inconsistent rule precision (some exact, some vague). Spread/leverage assumptions are dated to mid-2000s retail brokers and need re-validation against current costs.

## Who it is for

Beginner-to-intermediate forex day traders wanting a menu of concrete chart-and-indicator intraday setups to test on demo accounts, understanding none come with verified performance.

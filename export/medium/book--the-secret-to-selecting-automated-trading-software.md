# The Secret to Selecting Automated Trading Software: the trader's summary

*Short promotional article arguing that patience, low return expectations, and sticking with one system for 6-12 months matter more than any automated trading software.*

**Winsor A.G.A. Hoang** · Trading Psychology & Discipline · beginner

*Source coverage: full. PDF pages inspected: 1-2. These are study notes, not verified trading results.*

## Summary

A two-page opinion piece by Winsor A.G.A. Hoang, a forex system developer, arguing there is no "secret" to profitable automated trading beyond patience, discipline, and realistic return expectations. It warns readers away from marketing claims of huge guaranteed returns from cheap automated systems and frames automated software as a tool that can only capture opportunities the market actually offers, not a substitute for trading discipline.

## Key points

- Warns against chasing indicators or jumping between systems hoping to find a "Holy Grail."
- Recommends committing to one system for 6–12 months rather than switching frequently.
- Frames automated trading software as removing emotional execution errors (greed, fear) but not creating opportunities that don't exist in the market.
- Flags a specific red flag: if software claiming $5,000–$10,000/month potential is sold for only $295, treat the claim as implausible.
- Argues for keeping targeted returns low/modest, on the reasoning that a lower return target forces lower risk-taking and more consistency.
- Notes the article's author (a professional engineer) runs several of his own managed forex systems with published live results.

## Actionable rules

1. Commit to a single trading system or piece of software for 6–12 months before judging or abandoning it.
2. Treat any advertised return far above the price of the software (or above realistic professional returns) as a marketing red flag.
3. Target a modest, consistent return rather than an aggressive multiplier (e.g., turning $2,000 into $15,000 in two months) to keep position risk in check.

## Caveats

This is a short promotional/opinion piece with the author's own contact details and company (ctsforex.com) attached — it functions partly as marketing for the author's own managed trading systems, so its praise of "a profitable and consistent performing trading system" should be read with that conflict of interest in mind. No specific software evaluation criteria, backtest methodology, or concrete numbers are given beyond the general caution above.

## Who it is for

Beginners who are shopping for automated/EA trading software and need a basic skepticism checklist before buying or trusting marketing claims.

# Automated Forex EAs: the trader's summary

*A vendor essay recommends evaluating automated FX systems by fixed-lot live results, drawdown, stops, and diversification.*

**Winsor A. G. A. Hoang** · Trend Following & Mechanical Systems · intermediate

*Source coverage: full. PDF pages inspected: 1-2. These are study notes, not verified trading results.*

## Summary

Hoang argues that automated expert advisors are not a cure for poor trading and that one reckless trade without a stop can erase months of gains (p. 1). He urges skepticism toward claims of 100% wins or rapid account doubling, noting that compounded statements can hide risk. His due-diligence recommendation is to request at least six months of live data using fixed single-lot trading so profitability and drawdown are visible without compounding distortion (p. 1). After analysis, a trader should follow a selected system through drawdown rather than enter only during a winning streak. The author favors fixed stops and take profits larger than stops, and suggests running two systems on separate accounts where possible to diversify strategy exposure (p. 2).

## Key points

- Live fixed-lot history makes drawdown easier to compare across systems.
- Automation still carries model, execution, broker, and operational risk.

## Actionable rules

1. Request at least six months of live, fixed single-lot results and compare profit with drawdown; the author argues that compounded-lot statements obscure this relationship (pp. 1–2).
2. Reject 100%-win and rapid-doubling claims without evidence, and examine the risk responsible for the reported return (p. 1).
3. The source suggests entering a selected system during drawdown, using a fixed stop and a take-profit distance larger than the stop, and running two systems in separate accounts (p. 2). These vendor recommendations are not validated by results in the article.

## Caveats

The article is partly promotional and provides no independently audited data or definition of a “good” EA. Two systems may be highly correlated, so account separation does not guarantee diversification.

## Who it is for

Traders evaluating automated FX products.

# Staying Out of Trouble Trading Currencies with Channels: the trader's summary

*A compact currency workshop on confirming long trends and using channels to time lower-risk entries and exits.*

**Barbara Rockefeller** · 2000 · Swing Trading · intermediate

*Source coverage: full. PDF pages inspected: 1-28. These are study notes, not verified trading results.*

## Summary

Barbara Rockefeller's workshop argues that currency traders improve survival by spending less time exposed, trading less often, and requiring agreement among a small set of models. The presentation uses euro and sterling futures charts to distinguish a durable trend from tradable corrections. Its central device is an Average True Range channel: the longer trend supplies direction, while contact with the opposite channel boundary can refine entry. Momentum, moving averages, regression channels, and oscillators are supporting evidence rather than independent forecasting engines (PDF pp. 4-17).

## Key points

- Start with a long-horizon view, then inspect three timeframes before acting (PDF pp. 4-6).
- A 12/21 moving-average crossover and 11-day momentum illustrate confirmation; requiring agreement reduced the example's trades by about one-third (PDF pp. 6-8).
- Do not reverse on the first day of a fresh signal or anticipate an indicator turn (PDF p. 9).
- Channel breakouts need restraint: the workshop says more than 70% of the illustrated ATR breakouts were followed by a breakout the other way (PDF p. 11).
- Standard-error channels, Williams %R, patterns, and Fibonacci retracements are presented as checks on context, not as stand-alone commands (PDF pp. 12-15).

## Actionable rules

1. Establish direction with the slow trend model and seek confirmation from momentum before planning a trade.
2. In a downtrend, wait for price to reach or break the upper ATR band before entering short (PDF p. 17). A mirrored long setup is a research inference, not an explicit rule on that slide.
3. Avoid heavy exposure to the first breakout. The slide proposes doubling the position on a second upside breakout during the downtrend (PDF pp. 11, 17); it gives no complete risk budget for that increase.
4. Consider taking partial profit after price has remained outside the trend-side band for three days; that timing comes from the workshop's stated three-day average, not a general market law (PDF p. 17).

## Caveats

The available OCR across PDF pages 1-28 was reviewed, but several slides have missing text or unresolved chart detail, so coverage remains partial. Performance figures are historical illustrations without full transaction-cost or robustness analysis. The course supplies heuristics, not a completely specified system.

## Who it is for

Currency swing traders who already understand moving averages, momentum, and volatility channels.
